## Methodology check
- **Calib loader**: 200 wikitext-2 val sequences, len(strip)>50, truncation to SEQ_LEN=128, shape[1] ≥ SEQ_LEN. Same loader as cycles 218/258/259/260. PASS.
- **Token sample**: first 64 token positions of the first sequence. n_total_tokens captured = 25,600 (200 × 128) for the full b_leak source; ε_defect uses only the first 64 of these. PASS.
- **b_leak per block**: computed as `(F.gelu(z, approximate='tanh') - F.relu(z)).mean(dim=0)` over all 25,600 tokens, where `z = ln_2(out) @ W_fc + b_fc`. This matches cycle 258's recipe (verified by re-reading cycle 258's lines 119-129 — the formula is `(gelu_l - relu_l).mean(dim=0)`). PASS.
- **MLP forward**: `f(x) = (σ(x @ W_fc + b_fc)) @ W_proj + b_proj`. The c_proj.bias is included (correct — the agenda asks for the BLOCK MLP function, which in HF GPT-2 is `c_proj(act(c_fc(x)))` and includes both biases). PASS.
- **Jacobian**: `J = (W_fc * σ'(z).unsqueeze(0)) @ W_proj` → shape (768, 768). For GELU, σ'(z) is computed via fresh-leaf autograd (matches cycle 260 line 142). For ReLU+b_leak, σ'(z) = (z > 0).float() (the b_leak is an additive constant so its Jacobian contribution is zero). PASS.
- **Direction sampling**: 32 isotropic Gaussian unit directions sampled with `torch.Generator().manual_seed(42)`, normalized row-wise. Shared across all (l, σ, token) — correct for reproducibility. ‖H[0]‖ = 1.000000 confirmed. PASS.
- **Scale construction**: ε = frac × pullback_norm(x, U), where frac ∈ {0.01, 0.03, 0.10, 0.30}. The norm denominator is the *pullback* norm of x (G-norm of the residual stream input to the MLP), as the agenda specifies. PASS.
- **Vectorisation**: per (l, σ, token), J computed once, f(x) once, all 32 perturbed inputs batched into a single (32, 768) tensor and run through ONE forward through c_fc → activation → c_proj per scale → 4 scale-batches × N_TOKENS × N_BLOCKS × 2 σ = 6,144 batched forwards (each of size 32). Total forward time observed ~80s wall (run was longer due to model load and b_leak precompute). PASS.
- **G-norm**: residual ‖f(x+δ) − f(x) − Jδ‖_G and δ-norm ‖δ‖_G computed via `aleph.pullback_geometry.pullback_norm` which returns `‖v @ U.T‖_2` where U is the lm_head weight (50257 × 768). The denominator squared `‖δ‖²_G` is per-direction (different for each h since H_unit dirs in 768-D have heterogeneous G-norms — comment in code notes this). PASS.
- **n per (l, σ, scale)**: 64 tokens × 32 directions = 2,048. Confirmed in log: "n=2048" at every (l, σ, scale) bucket. PASS.

## Numerical sanity
- **n_total_probes** = 64 × 32 × 4 × 12 × 2 = 196,608 ε_defect evaluations. Matches the agenda's headline count. PASS.
- **b_leak polarity**: per-block min/max all negative or near-zero (block 0 mean −0.067 min −0.118 max +0.000; trend matches cycle 258's reported polarity ranges of [−0.111, −0.091] mean). The cycle 263 b_leak min/max per-block printed in the log are within rounding distance of cycle 258's values — the b_leak source is the same and the math is identical. PASS.
- **Per-direction ‖δ‖_G heterogeneity**: each of the 32 directions has its own pullback norm (because h is sampled in standard 768-d space, then U.T projects each direction differently in vocab space). The denominator's per-direction variance produces variance in ε_defect even across directions of the same Euclidean norm. The aggregation over 32 directions × 64 tokens reports median, p90, p99 → robust to this heterogeneity. PASS.
- **Sub-quadratic scaling observed**: median ε(scale) DECREASES monotonically with ε_scale at every (l, σ). e.g. GELU block 11: 0.804 → 0.585 → 0.253 → 0.093. For a purely quadratic f(x) the ratio ‖residual‖/‖h‖² should be CONSTANT in ‖h‖. The factor-of-12 decrease over the 30× scale range means residual ‖f(x+h) − f(x) − J h‖_G grows ~LINEARLY in ‖h‖, not quadratically. This is a STRONG observation: f(x) is *less* than quadratically curved at the relevant scales, which is the cleanest geometric reason the activation choice doesn't matter much (the second-order Taylor term is sub-dominant for both σ types). The slopes match closely between GELU and ReLU+b_leak (GELU block 11: 0.804 → 0.093 ratio 8.6; ReLU+b_leak block 11: 0.713 → 0.091 ratio 7.8 — within 10% of each other). The hypothesis's "GELU defect monotonically quadratic in ‖h‖" claim is therefore ALSO falsified — neither activation produces a clean quadratic profile. PASS.
- **Block 11 magnitude dominance**: at the smallest scale, GELU block 11 median = 0.804 vs blocks 1-9 medians ~0.08-0.13 — block 11 is ~10× larger. This is consistent with block 11 being immediately before LN_f / lm_head; its c_proj output direction is structurally different from intermediate blocks. Block 0 also elevated (median 0.146 vs 0.077 at block 1). Geometrically: blocks 0 and 11 are the boundary blocks (post-embedding and pre-readout) where the residual stream geometry differs from the deep stack. PASS.
- **Hypothesis's qualitative direction is correct, magnitude is not**: the hypothesis predicted "p99 ε_R > p99 ε_G in early blocks 0-4 because ReLU has hyperplane crossings". The direction is exactly right (5/5 of blocks 0-4 have p99 R/G ≥ 1), but the magnitudes are 1.02-1.22 — a 2-22% effect, NOT a 5× / spike-dominated effect. The b_leak smoothing kills the dramatic kink-spikes that an uncorrected ReLU would produce. Note (i): the cycle 258 b_leak is a *first-moment* correction at the 3072-channel intermediate layer; it does NOT remove the elementwise non-differentiability of ReLU at z=0, so the σ' is still discontinuous. But because the per-channel ‖W_proj column‖ is bounded and the discontinuity is smeared by the integration over 3072 channels and the random direction sampling (the chance that a random unit vector in R^768 maps through W_fc to a z that crosses any specific hyperplane is small but not zero), the effect on the *block function*'s ε_defect is geometrically washed out. The hypothesis's intuition was correct at the activation function level but wrong at the block-function level. PASS.
- **REFUTED branch's stated alternative is empirically supported**: the agenda's REFUTED clause text says "the FFN block's nonlinearity is dominated by something other than the per-channel activation function (e.g. W_up stretches dwarf the elementwise nonlinearity contribution)". The data confirm exactly this reading — both σ types produce nearly identical block-function curvature, so the linear envelope (W_fc and W_proj) controls the geometry of f(x) much more than the choice of σ. PASS.
- **Cycle 260 consistency**: cycle 260 found ΔJ = J_GELU − J_ReLU has medium rank (~335/768) and Frobenius norm growing 80 → 192 with depth, but its mode-1 directions ARE strongly aligned with the next block's c_fc reader (top-5 cos sum 2.5-3.5). The cycle 263 finding that block-function ε_defect is σ-insensitive is consistent: the operator-level disagreement IS real and structured, but it lives in a medium-rank subspace that, when integrated through the W_proj averaging, produces a near-identical per-direction quadratic-form magnitude. The two cycles together say: "GELU and ReLU disagree about *which* directions are stretched (cycle 260 alignment), but they agree about *how much* per-direction curvature each input gets (cycle 263 magnitude)." PASS.

## Hypothesis vs result
- **Hypothesis**: GELU is everywhere mildly curved, ReLU is bimodal (mostly flat with rare hyperplane spikes). Median ε_G > median ε_R at every block; p99 ε_R > p99 ε_G in early blocks where ReLU spikes dominate the tail.
- **Reality**:
  - **Median G/R**: ranges from 0.75 to 1.13, NOT > 1 at every block (5/12 blocks have median G/R < 1, including 4 of the 5 early blocks). Hypothesis's "median G > median R at every block" is FALSE for early blocks.
  - **p99 R/G**: 5/5 of early blocks 0-4 have p99 R/G > 1 (range 1.02-1.22), so the *direction* of the prediction is correct, but the magnitudes (≤22%) are 5-25× smaller than the implied "fat-tail spikes" picture would require to clear the agenda's CONFIRMED threshold (which only required p99 R/G ≥ 1 — a trivial bound that the data clears, but the COMBINED A+B clauses cannot pass because A fails by 4.97×).
  - Ratios cluster within ±27% of unity at all blocks for both medians and p99 — well inside the [0.5, 2] REFUTED tolerance band.
- **Geometric reading**: the per-channel σ choice does NOT control the block MLP function's local quadratic-form magnitude. The c_fc and c_proj linear maps wrapping the elementwise nonlinearity dominate the geometry; the b_leak first-moment correction further smooths what little curvature distinction would otherwise show up. The hypothesis was correct at the activation-function level (gelu''(z) is smooth and nonzero everywhere; relu''(z) is a delta at z=0) but wrong at the block-function level. The block function f(x) = c_proj(σ(c_fc(x))) is about as locally nonlinear under either σ.
- **Counterintuitive check** (CLAUDE.md rule): is REFUTED a surprise? The cycle 258 finding that all-12-block ReLU+b_leak swap moves κ by ≤50% relative to GELU baseline already hinted that the σ choice perturbs the block function only modestly. The cycle 260 finding that ΔJ is medium-rank (335/768) and broadly distributed (60-70% of 3072 channels active) reinforces the picture: the σ disagreement is structured but distributed, not concentrated in a few hyperplane-crossing spikes. So REFUTED is the *expected* extension of cycles 258/260 to the second-order curvature measurement — this cycle confirms the same picture at a different geometric order.
- **Sub-quadratic scaling is the bonus finding**: even *within* a single σ type, the local linearity defect grows ~linearly in ‖h‖ rather than quadratically. This means the per-block MLP function f is "more linear than expected" at the calibration-input scales used here. Possible explanations: (a) the residual ‖f(x+h) − f(x) − Jh‖ is dominated by the *third-order* Taylor term at these scales, not the second-order term — the Hessian operator is small relative to the third derivative; (b) the random-direction sampling tends to land in directions where the block function is locally near-linear (e.g. directions orthogonal to W_fc's principal subspace, where the σ' is roughly constant); (c) the f(x+h) − f(x) − Jh formula has a zero second-order contribution by construction whenever σ'(z) is locally constant (the Hessian of f w.r.t. x is `W_fc^T diag(σ''(z) · h_proj) W_proj` which is small in directions where h doesn't move z through σ's curved region). All three are plausible; the cleanest follow-up is (b): re-run with directions sampled in the W_fc principal subspace to see if the quadratic profile recovers.
