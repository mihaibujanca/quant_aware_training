Two geometric findings: (i) **Cycle 270's weight-SVD ≠ activation-SVD
lesson generalises.** Cycle 278 measured block 1 c_proj OUTPUT eff_rank=1.94
(top-1 activation energy 92.94%). Cycle 280 measures the WEIGHT SVD: top-2
covers only 7.57% of the weight-energy, and rank-2 no-retrain still costs
+3.76 PPL (NOT ≤1 as the brief predicted). The activation eff_rank is a
property of the joint action of c_fc + c_proj on the input distribution,
NOT of c_proj.weight alone. Second independent confirmation. (ii) **Adam
recovery per param is MUCH higher at block 1 than block 11.** Cycle 270:
block 11 rank-22 retrain recovered 59.68 → 54.47 = -5.22 PPL with 17,664
params. Cycle 280: block 1 rank-2 retrain recovers 57.66 → 49.03 = -8.63 PPL
with 2,304 params. Per-param effectiveness is ~25.7× higher at block 1 —
eff_rank IS directionally predictive of retrained compressibility at the
single-block c_proj weight-SVD level, even though it does NOT predict
no-retrain compressibility. This is a refinement of cycle 273's "eff_rank
not prescriptive at cascade level": at 1-block c_proj weight-SVD + Adam
retrain, eff_rank-ordered candidates ARE predictive of post-retrain quality.
