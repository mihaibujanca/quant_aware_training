                   rank-2 retrain best gap from float = +0.63% (within 1 std).

Structural difference: in cycle 296, layers 7-11 (after l*) provide downstream
capacity to absorb the rank-truncation residual. In cycle 298, l*=3 is the last
hidden layer; only model.layers[8] = Linear(1, 256) is downstream — too narrow
to absorb a 256-dim residual. This is a structural rather than empirical claim.

still improving when budget ran out. Recorded ratios are upper bounds on what
500 Adam steps achieve; true asymptotic ratios may be lower. The verdict logic
is independent of budget-saturation (operates on retrain_best_held_mse regardless),
so this is a substantive caveat but not a verdict correction. Same finding as
cycle 280, 287, 294 budget-bounded results on transformer LASER.
