  damaged. b1's 2-direction subspace cannot absorb the additional perturbation from
  b10's 5 trainable directions interacting through 9 frozen blocks of GPT-2.

- Joint Adam over 6,912 params cannot find a basin that respects both anchors
  simultaneously when the perturbation magnitude (b10 r=5 init PPL gap from float
  is 6.0 PPL, vs b1 r=2 gap of 3.7 PPL) overwhelms b1's recovery capacity.

The "two weight-SVD low-rank patches at non-adjacent depths compose non-destructively
the way cycle 284 did for blocks 1 and 11" hypothesis is REFUTED. The compositional
property of cycle 284 does NOT generalize to (b1, b10) — the depth-related dependency
is real, but it interacts with rank capacity, not just depth distance. b10 r=5 is
"too much" perturbation for b1 r=2 to absorb.
