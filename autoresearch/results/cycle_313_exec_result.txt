== Substantive findings sharper than INCONCLUSIVE ==

1. **Calibration HELPS but does not close the gap.** Item 323's REFUTED
   bar ("calibration doesn't help, the +8.63 retrain delta is genuinely
   from Adam fitting a non-SVD optimum") is technically rejected by the
   data (74/84 cells improve), but the SUBSTANTIVE reading is that
   calibration closes only ~12% of the b1 gap (~1 PPL of ~8.6 PPL). The
   remaining 88% of the gap is genuinely from Adam non-SVD fitting.

2. **Cycle 280's "init was suboptimal" hypothesis is PARTIALLY validated
   but quantitatively inadequate.** The init IS suboptimal compared to
   weighted SVD (57.66 -> 56.70 at b1 r=2), but the improvement is too
   small to call cycle 280 a "bug". The trained Adam optimum at 49.03
   is genuinely far from any SVD basis -- weighted or not.

3. **b2 is the strongest calibration win.** b2 cal r=2 = 57.83 vs unc
   r=2 = 64.08 (delta -6.26 PPL). b2's c_proj input has a highly
   non-isotropic covariance (cycle 311 reported b2 r=1 trained = 49.75
   = b2 r=2 trained 49.33 within seed variation, suggesting the b2 c_proj
   spectrum is rank-1 dominated). Weighted SVD whitens the input
   directions and recovers the rank-2 truncation that cycle 285's
   uncentered SVD missed by 6.26 PPL. This is the clearest evidence that
   SVD-LLM weighting is the right correction at the BLOCK level, even
   though it cannot reach trained-Adam quality.

4. **b0 is uniquely catastrophic.** No published cycle 280-class
   single-block experiment has targeted b0 because c_proj_0 SVD
   truncation breaks the model at any rank tested. Calibration provides
   a 4.9x improvement at r=64 (439 -> 89) but absolute PPL is still
   unusable. Cycle 278 reports b0 c_proj output eff_rank = 71.93
   (HIGHEST of all 12 blocks); the no-train SVD truncation below eff_rank
   breaks the model. c_proj_0 operates on the post-LayerNorm embedding
   activations and may be structurally different from c_proj_{1..11}.

5. **b11 is the unique calibration LOSER.** Mean delta +0.10 PPL,
   r=1 +1.10 PPL (worst). b11 is the latest residual-stream concentrator
   (cycle 270/304/310/311 all flag it as unique). One hypothesis: the
   200-seq calibration set under-represents the late-layer activation
   distribution (residual stream is most diverse late). Another: b11's
   SVD top-rank singular axes are already nearly aligned with the input
   covariance, so weighted SVD adds no new signal.

6. **r=64 is the best cell at every block (b1..b11).** Both variants
   monotone-decrease in PPL with r (with one exception: b1 unc r=16
   slightly above r=8). At r=64 calibrated, b1..b11 achieve 54.91-58.44
   PPL -- only 1.0-4.5 PPL above float. The SVD-LLM literature claim
   of "no-training works at small accuracy cost" is TRUE for r=64
   (~4 PPL above float, no retraining) but FALSE for r=2 (~4-9 PPL
   above float, AND ~7 PPL gap to trained-Adam).

7. **The rank elbow is at r=8..16.** Per-r decreases per rank doubling:
   r=1->2: ~0.1-0.7 PPL improvement
   r=2->4: ~0.1-1.4 PPL
   r=4->8: ~0.2-1.0 PPL
   r=8->16: ~0.0-0.6 PPL
   r=16->32: ~0.5-0.9 PPL
   r=32->64: ~0.4-1.1 PPL
   The slope is small at r=2..16 then steepens at r=32..64 -- inverse of
   the usual "low-rank elbow". This is because at very low r (1..4) the
   SVD truncation is far from optimal regardless of basis; at moderate
   r (8..32) the truncation captures most of the signal; at high r (64)
   only the noise tail is dropped.

8. **Cross-check: cycle 313 published-init reproductions are exact.**
   - b1 r=2 uncentered = 57.6581 vs cycle 280 published 57.664: |delta| = 0.006 (PASS)
   - b2 r=1 uncentered = 72.3808 vs cycle 285 published 72.3808: |delta| = 0.0000 (PASS)

   The cycle 313 SVD pipeline is bit-identical to cycle 280-class single-

9. **Substantive verdict (not the literal verbatim verdict)**: cycle
   280's +8.63 PPL retrain delta at b1 r=2 is mostly real, not a
   calibration bug. Weighted SVD closes 12% of the gap (1.0 PPL). The
   remaining 88% is from Adam fitting a non-SVD-orthogonal direction in
   weight space. Cycle 280's "Adam fits a non-SVD optimum" reading is
