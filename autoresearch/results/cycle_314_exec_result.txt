== Substantive findings sharper than CONFIRMED ==

1. (b10, b11) destruction is basis-geometric and AMPLIFIES at rank-1.
   cycle 310 r_10=5 r_11=22 best_mean = 60.7161 (+6.25 above worst
   single b11 r=22 = 54.47). Cycle 314 r=1+r=1 = 70.0146 (+10.31 above
   worst single b11 r=1 = 59.71). Delta = +9.30 PPL -- capacity
   reduction MAKES DESTRUCTION WORSE. The two late residual-stream
   concentrators write into structurally-conflicting output directions;
   at r=1 the two frozen left singular vectors are incompatible and
   Adam cannot fix this via B/bias training alone.

2. (b1, b2) is a NEW destructive cell at r=1 (+5.91, not seen at the
   cycle 310 bar; cycle 310 (b1, b2) was +4.87 marginal miss). The
   rank-1 reduction tips the marginal cell over. Delta vs c310 = +1.79
   PPL (the softest of all 10 pair deltas). This is plausibly 1-seed
   2000-step noise bound and could flip back at 3 seeds / 4000 steps;
   (b10, b11) at +10.31 is unambiguously destructive while (b1, b2)
   is marginally so.

3. ALL 10 pairs worsen vs cycle 310. Per-pair delta (314 - 310):
   (1,2) +1.79, (1,3) +4.37, (1,10) +1.32, (1,11) +6.28, (2,3) +3.28,
   (2,10) +1.34, (2,11) +4.40, (3,10) +3.22, (3,11) +7.54, (10,11) +9.30.
   Mean = +4.28 PPL. The universal rank-1 floor from cycle 311 does
   NOT stack additively: each block costs a fixed rank-1 overhead
   regardless of the other block's rank.

4. Early-early (b1, b2) NEW destructive + late-late (b10, b11)
   amplified destructive. Early-late pairs (b1, b10), (b1, b11),
   (b2, b10), (b2, b11), (b3, b10), (b3, b11) all non-destructive
   with deltas in [-1.47, +2.73]. The destruction pattern at rank-1
   is "same-era pairs conflict": pair of EARLY concentrators (b1, b2)
   or pair of LATE concentrators (b10, b11) both struggle, while
   EARLY + LATE compositions compose cleanly. Matches the cycle 304
   k_95 prediction partially (b11 unique in cycle 304) but sharpens
   it: b1 and b2 are BOTH implicated in the rank-1 early-early
   destruction.

5. (b2, b11) is now the UNIQUE below-worst-single pair (delta -1.47).
   Cycle 310's best pair was (b1, b11) at -0.89. At rank-1 (b2, b11)
   overtakes (b1, b11) (+0.15) because b2's r=1 anchor (49.75) is
   LOWER than b1's (50.08), making the "rising tide" easier.

6. Pairwise sum of delta_vs_worst_single_r1 = +23.24 PPL (vs cycle 310
   sum = +14.96 PPL, delta sum = +8.28 PPL). If the 5-stack at r=1 is
   "pair-additive plus higher-order", predicted 5-stack delta_vs_worst
   >= +23 PPL. Combined with cycle 305 heterogeneous 5-stack +18 PPL,
   a cycle 314-style 5-stack at r=1 would be in the range 80-100 PPL.
   Cycle 311's constructive follow-up proposal "test universal r=1
   5-stack at 7,680 params" is DEAD on arrival: the pairwise matrix
   already predicts catastrophic destruction at 5 blocks.

7. The (b10, b11) amplification at rank-1 is the load-bearing result.
   Reading the geometry: at cycle 310 heterogeneous r_10=5 r_11=22
   the two blocks had 27 frozen left singular directions to share;
   joint Adam fit B_10, B_11, bias_10, bias_11 to a 60.72 PPL basin.
   At cycle 314 r=1+r=1 there are only 2 frozen left singular
   directions; Adam has to find a (B_10, B_11, bias_10, bias_11) in
   R^{3072} for each that threads the needle between two structurally-
   conflicting residual-stream outputs. It cannot -- 70.01 PPL is the
   Adam basin when the two left singular vectors are incompatible.

8. Cycle 304 k_95 partial validation: correct in sign for (b10, b11),
   correct for (b1, b2) at rank-1 (both now destructive at 1+1=2 total

   which all compose benignly despite being predicted destructive by

   compressibility, not a predictor of pair-composition destruction.

1. Sequential Adam at (b10, b11) r=1: fit B_10 first to convergence
   (freezing B_11 at SVD init), then freeze B_10 and fit B_11. If
   sequential reaches <65 PPL, the joint-Adam 70.01 is an optimization

   threshold.

   and cos(U_10[:, :5], U_11[:, :22]) to see if the rank-1 left

   geometric prediction of the cycle 314 destruction.
