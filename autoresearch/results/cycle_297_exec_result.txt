Substantive finding (the result the verbatim verdict cannot fully capture):
  Training does NOT collapse eff_rank on this 2D-input testbed -- it LIFTS it. In all 3
  seeds, the per-layer trajectory is upward (final > init in every layer or essentially
  equal). The "collapse to rank-2" framing has the WRONG SIGN here:
    - layers START at eff_rank ~ 1.0 (already collapsed by ReLU contraction on 2D input;
      cycle 295 / Hanin-Rolnick N^d bound, lit map line 594)
    - training mildly TWISTS the activation manifold UP to eff_rank ~ 1.2 - 2.8

  None of the 3 seeds is monotone non-increasing across the run. Max single-window
  relative drop across ALL 3 seeds * 12 layers = 0.325 (seed 42 layer 5), well below the
  0.50 phase-transition threshold. The "phase transition vs gradual" question on this
  testbed collapses to "did anything go DOWN?" Answer: not really.

  Only CONFIRMED-init-rank fires. The substantive answer to item 307's question on this
  testbed is: the collapse happens at random initialization. By the time training starts,
  layers 0-2 sit at eff_rank 1.7-3.3 and layers 3-11 sit at 1.0-1.3. Training pulls the
  deeper layers UP by 0.1-1.5 over 12000 epochs, never down.

  This confirms (and extends) cycle 295's substantive reading via the trajectory itself.
  Cycle 278's GPT-2 b1/b2 collapse to eff_rank 1.94/1.05 cannot be studied via this
  testbed because the testbed has no opportunity to collapse -- it starts collapsed.

  Implications for next planner:
  - The 2D-input spirals/moons testbed is unusable for trajectory analysis on cycle 278's
    question. It is the wrong control. Stop trying to use it for that purpose.
  - Principled follow-up A: rerun item 307 with a higher-dim input lift (100D embedding
    from MEMORY.md, or random 32D linear lift) so init eff_rank can plausibly be > 5 and
    actually drop during training.
  - Principled follow-up B (better, IMO): instrument GPT-2 directly during cycle 280's
    4000-step Adam retrain to see whether eff_rank moves at all during the LASER fine-tune
    that produced the cycle 280 result. This is the trajectory question on the actual
    object of interest, not a 2D toy proxy.
