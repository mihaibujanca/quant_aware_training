== Interpretation (substantive reading) ==
1. Cycle 302's E_kept_top_2 framing was where the energy MASS is, not
   where it TERMINATES. The curvature residual has a long Gaussian-like
   tail at every block except 11. Concentrator blocks at k=2 (E_top2
   >= 0.8) were picked by "max 2D mass" criterion, but only block 11
   actually TERMINATES before k=10.

2. Only 1/12 blocks (block 11) reaches k_95 <= 10. The other 11 span
   k_95 in [63, 542], with median k_95 ~ 337. 11/12 blocks are in the
   REFUTED bucket (k_95 >= 50).

3. "Concentrator" blocks from item 314's definition (1, 2, 10, 11) split
   sharply:
     - b11 : k_95 = 2   (clean rank-2 concentration; spectrum is
                         dominant-s1-heavy, s1=61 vs s2=34.6 ratio 1.76x)
     - b2  : k_95 = 63  (best among non-b11, but still 12.6x the <=5 bar)
     - b10 : k_95 = 397
     - b1  : k_95 = 348
   The "concentrator" label, motivated by E_top2 >= 0.75, was an artifact
   of the cycle 302 rank-2 measurement and does NOT translate to k_95.

4. Mid-block 8: item 314 predicted k_95 in [8, 15]. Measured 511. This is
   the FLATTEST spectrum (E_top1 = 0.44 vs concentrator E_top1 ~ 0.90)
   combined with a heavy tail. Confirms item 316's block-8 deep dive
   hypothesis that b8 has BOTH a flat W_proj AND a broad curvature
   residual.

5. Block 0: item 314 predicted k_95 >= 20. Measured 445. This is
   consistent with cycle 278's eff_rank(c_proj_0) = 71.93 high measurement
   AND cycle 217 rank_95(block 0) = 10 for raw Dy (Dy itself is
   low-rank, but its alignment with the top right-SV subspace of W_proj
   is spread because W_proj at b0 is close to isotropic: s1=25, s2=21,
   s3=18, s4=17, s5=16 -- flat top-5).

6. Block 11 is the UNIQUE true low-rank curvature block. Cycles 280/285/
   288/289/292 single-block LASER anchors at b1/b2/b3/b10/b11 remain
   valid (those were retrained rank selections, not this kind of
   termination measurement), but the "rank-2 terminator" story only
   holds for b11. The recipe-anchor Pareto tiles (cycle 284 b1 r=2 +
   b11 r=22, cycle 292 b1 r=1) are not invalidated -- they're training-
   based floors, not k_95 floors. But item 314's claim "rank-5 essentially
   complete at concentrators" is REFUTED: rank 5 captures only 77-92% of
   curvature at b1/b2/b10, far from 95%.

7. Geometric restatement: for every block except 11, the curvature
   residual Dy = delta @ W_proj is a dense vector inside R^768 with a
   long tail in the top-k right-singular subspace of W_proj. Cycle 301/
   302's "rank-2 concentration" is that the TOP-2 components contain
   47-98% of the energy. This cycle adds: the REMAINING 2-53% of the
   energy is spread over a broad subspace (dense tail), so achieving
   95% recovery costs k ~ 50-550 ranks depending on the block.
