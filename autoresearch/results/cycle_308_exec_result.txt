Substantive findings sharper than REFUTED:
1. Cycle 300's "unigram axes" reading is a TOKEN-LIST property
   (top-20 boost tokens are stopwords) of the trained B factor's
   projection through the LM head W_E. Cycle 308 tests whether the
   underlying R^768 direction is RECOVERABLE from W_E^T @ log_p
   and W_E^T @ s_vec. It is not: at unit magnitude (s1=s2=1) the
   synthetic gives 577.69 vs 57.66 for the SVD init at the SAME
   magnitude (10x worse).

2. The directional gap survives the 25-point scale grid: best is
   127.50 at (0.5, 0.5), still 70 PPL worse than the SVD init.
   The lower-left corner location of the optimum suggests even
   smaller scales might recover some PPL, but the brief specifies
   a fixed grid with no fit beyond it. Even if scaled to zero, the
   replacement would converge to the bias-only baseline, which is
   strictly worse than the SVD init (which contains a real W_proj
   contribution along Vh[:2, :]).

3. d2 (the stopword-indicator axis) is the dominant damage source.
   At fixed s2=0.5, increasing s1 from 0.5 to 5.0 inflates PPL
   from 128 to 52,486 (410x). At fixed s1=0.5, increasing s2 from
   0.5 to 10.0 inflates PPL from 128 to 25,434 (199x). Both
   directions are GEOMETRICALLY OFF; the failure is symmetric in
   sign even though the magnitudes differ.

4. The "trained B factor projection looks like stopwords" property
   has a mechanistic alternative explanation: many R^768 vectors
   project (via wte) onto a top-20 token list dominated by high
   prior-frequency tokens (stopwords) because of LM-head geometry,
   not because the source vector was a unigram axis. Cycle 308 is
   the discriminating test, and it selects this reading.

5. Compression implication: the rank-2 floor at block 1 cannot be
   reached with a model-agnostic construction; data-driven fitting
   (Adam OR SVD of W_proj itself) is the irreducible step. Cycle
   280's anchor remains valid as a TRAINED rank-2 result, not a
   hand-designed one.
