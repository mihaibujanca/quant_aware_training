== Substantive finding ==

Training does NOT collapse eff_rank on this 2D-input testbed — it LIFTS it. In all 3 seeds,
the per-layer trajectory is upward (final > init in every layer). The "collapse to rank-2"
framing has the wrong sign here:
  - layers START at eff_rank ~ 1.0 (already collapsed at random init by ReLU contraction
    on a 2D input; cycle 295 / Hanin-Rolnick N^d bound)
  - training mildly TWISTS the activation manifold up to eff_rank ~ 1.2 - 2.8

None of the 3 seeds is monotone non-increasing across the run (False for all three).
Max single-window relative drop across ALL layers and ALL seeds = 0.325 (seed 42, layer 5),
well below the 0.50 phase-transition threshold. CONFIRMED-phase clause does not fire.
CONFIRMED-gradual clause requires monotone non-increasing AND no >=50% drop; the first
condition fails on every seed (the curves go UP). REFUTED clause requires "doesn't collapse
at all" — the changes are real but upward, not downward, so REFUTED doesn't fire either.

Only CONFIRMED-init-rank fires. The substantive answer to item 307's question ("when does
the collapse happen?") on this testbed: at random initialization. By the time training
starts, layers 0-2 sit at eff_rank 1.7-3.3 and layers 3-11 sit at 1.0-1.3. Training pulls
layers 3-11 up by 0.1-1.5 over 12000 epochs, never down. The "phase transition vs gradual"
distinction is moot; there is no collapse to characterize.

This is the same conclusion as cycle 295's init census, now confirmed by the trajectory:
the 2D input completely dominates the eff_rank profile. Cycle 278's GPT-2 b1/b2 collapse
to eff_rank 1.94/1.05 cannot be studied via this testbed because there is no opportunity
to collapse — the testbed starts collapsed.

Implication for cycle 278's training-dynamics question: the spirals testbed is unusable
for trajectory analysis. The principled follow-up is a higher-dim input lift (100D
embedding from MEMORY.md), or — better — instrument GPT-2 directly during cycle 280's
4000-step Adam retrain to see whether eff_rank moves at all during the LASER fine-tune
that produced the cycle 280 result.
