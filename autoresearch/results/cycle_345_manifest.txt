## Interpretation
K* = 25597 out of 25600 tokens at EVERY block (99.99% unique). The activation
pattern space is not clustered at all — each token lands in its own piecewise-
linear cell. K* scales with n_tokens, not with width, so more data would only
yield more unique patterns. Piecewise-linear approximation with K << n_tokens
pieces cannot faithfully represent these MLPs. The MASO/spline theory's
"few regions" hope does not hold for this model: ~30% of neurons active per
token means ~2^(0.3*1920) = 2^576 reachable cells, far more than any dataset.
