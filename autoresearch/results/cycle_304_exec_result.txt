   blocks where the operator IS genuinely low-rank, the curvature
   residual is not -- it has a broad tail.

4. Cycles 280/285/288/289/292 single-block LASER anchors (b1 r=2,
   b2 r=2, b3 r=24, b10 r=5, b11 r=22) are NOT invalidated. Those were
   TRAINING-BASED rank selections (Adam closes the delta from init via
   the output bias and row-space rotation in B; cycle 299's 83% basin
   reading). k_95 is a very different object: it's the rank needed for
   95% of the curvature residual energy to pass through a top-k
   right-SV projection of W_proj, with NO Adam rotation. A training
   recovery of 8-15 PPL can come from a low-rank subspace that captures
   75% of the curvature energy IF the remaining 25% is absorbed by the
   bias + row rotation (cycle 299 finding). k_95 is the rank needed if
   you demand 95% energy projected, not 95% PPL recovery.
