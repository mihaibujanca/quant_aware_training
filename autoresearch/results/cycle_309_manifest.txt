== Substantive findings sharper than CONFIRMED ==

(1) The verbatim threshold passes by a wide margin (54.32 vs 60.0; gap = 5.7
    PPL), but the CONFIRMED branch's interpretive claim ("Δy basis is the
    right basis at b8") is rejected by the paired baseline.

(2) wproj wins at both r=1 (+1.02 PPL) and r=2 (+0.75 PPL). std across seeds
    is tiny (≤ 0.013), so the ordering is robust.

(3) The cycle 306 "53% unprojected" framing measures Δy's energy fraction
    captured by span(Vh_w[:2]) on the 768-dim *output* side. Constraining
    c_proj's row space to span(Vh_y[:r]) on the *output* side is the correct
    operational reading of "Δy basis", but it kills 98%+ of W_proj's
    *input-side* energy (the cross-basis sanity above), and the trainable
    factor B (r, 768) cannot recover what was thrown away on the input side.

(4) Both bases recover from the same ~61 PPL no-retrain init to within
    [52.85, 54.32] under Adam. The basis difference influences only the
    direction the trainable parameters can search; A_w and A_y span different
    r-dim subspaces of R^3072. wproj's A_w is a rank-r operator on W_proj's
    highest-energy *input* directions, while dy's A_y is a rank-r operator
    on Δy's specific *output* directions, which point into W_proj's
    *low-energy input tail*.

(5) Implication for cycle 305 destructive composition: the cycle 306
    constructive follow-up was based on the assumption that the b8 anomaly
    was a basis-choice problem. This cycle shows the assumption is wrong
    at the comparative level. Cycle 305's destructive composition at b8
    is NOT explained by "the LASER kept the wrong basis"; the LASER kept
    the *better* basis. The b8 contribution to multi-block destruction
    must be reframed.

(6) Δy is rank-1 dominated INTRINSICALLY (top1_E=0.91, stable rank=1.20),
    but this rank-1 axis is in W_proj's tail. Rank-1 dominance of Δy on
    its own SVD does not transfer into rank-1 capacity for the c_proj
    operator: the operator's input-side rank is determined by W_proj's
    spectrum, not by Δy's.

(7) Across all 12 (cell, seed) pairs, best_step = 4000 (training still
    descending at the budget end). Absolute PPLs are *upper bounds* on
    the trainable floor for each cell. The wproj > dy ordering is robust
    to budget extension; both cells' descent slopes are essentially
    parallel (the 0.5--1 PPL separation persists for the last ~1500
    steps).

(8) Cross-validation with prior cycles:
    - Float PPL = 53.8949 matches cycle 270/280/305 reference 53.895 to <0.001.
    - Δy top1_E at b8 = 0.9141 matches cycle 306 reference 0.9141 to 4 dp.
    - W_proj top-5 S at b8 = [31.08, 24.49, 18.53, 16.77, 16.47] matches
      cycle 306 reference exactly.
    - LowRankCProj template is the cycle 270/280/292 template; bit-for-bit
      identical to cycle 280's no-retrain init for the wproj branch (init
      PPL == no-retrain PPL within 1e-2 asserted per seed).
