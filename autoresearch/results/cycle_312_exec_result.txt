== Substantive findings sharper than INCONCLUSIVE ==

1. **Width buys exactly 4 more layers of init rank memory.** Cycle 307 (hidden
   =32, entropy eff_rank) had init collapse to ~1 by L4; cycle 312 (hidden=128,
   stable rank) has init collapse to ~1 by L8 (L5 init=1.88, L7 init=1.10,
   L8 init=1.04). The 2.56x wider network pushes the Hanin-Rolnick activation-
   pattern bottleneck 4 layers deeper. The collapse depth is roughly
   log(hidden / input_dim) layers past input_dim -- width helps but only
   logarithmically. Cycle 307's reading "hidden_width=32 is the binding
   constraint past layer 0" is VALIDATED for width=32 and also shown to be
   INSUFFICIENT at width=128: the bottleneck just moves, it does not go away.

2. **Init eff_rank at L0 = 69.17 (not 128).** Random Gaussian ReLU on a 50D
   input does NOT yield stable-rank 128 at L0 even when width >> input_dim.
   The layer 0 activation matrix Y_0 = ReLU(X W_0^T + b_0) has stable rank
   ~70, reflecting the combined input rank 50 + ReLU half-space killing. The
   upper bound 128 is never hit because the signal dimensionality entering L0
   is 50, and the 128 rows of W_0 oversample that 50D subspace.

3. **Training COLLAPSES eff_rank at layers 1-4 and LIFTS it at layers 5-11.**
   The sign of the training-induced change flips at depth 5:
     L1: 50.39 -> 18.69 (down by 31.7)
     L2: 31.15 -> 5.10  (down by 26.0)
     L3: 13.05 -> 2.77  (down by 10.3)
     L4:  4.23 -> 2.35  (down by 1.9)
     L5:  1.88 -> 2.20  (UP by 0.3)
     L6:  1.30 -> 2.08  (UP by 0.8)
     ...
     L11: 1.00 -> 1.85  (UP by 0.85)
   Training takes the high-rank early layers DOWN and the near-collapsed late
   layers UP. The bidirectional motion toward ~2 post eff_rank at the deep
   layers is the same sign as cycle 297 (spirals, hidden=32) and cycle 307
   (50D, hidden=32). Width does not change the qualitative training dynamic
   at the deep layers.

4. **Cycle 278 GPT-2 bathtub (b1=1.94, b2=1.05) is NOT reproduced.** Item 322
   asked whether hidden=128 unblocks the cycle 307 failure to reproduce the
   bathtub. Cycle 312's post eff_rank at the deep layers is ~2.0 for ALL
   layers 5-11 (not 1.05 at any single layer, nor 195 anywhere). The GPT-2
   bathtub is a NON-MONOTONE profile (deep valley around b1-b2, high values
   around b5-b9) that this ReLU MLP testbed cannot reproduce. Cycle 312's
   post profile is MONOTONE DECREASING from 67 (L0) down to ~2 (L3+) with a
   slight dip at L11 (1.85). No bathtub at any width tested.

5. **Trajectory is non-monotone because e=50 overshoots the plateau.** The
   early-layer eff_rank ATTAINS a local minimum at e=50 then rebounds to a
   stable value at e>=200. Example L2: e=0 31.15, e=50 4.82, e=200 5.12, e=500
   5.11, e=2000 5.11 -- the e=50 dip of 0.3 below the plateau breaks the
   monotone-decreasing count. The cond_b verdict fails on a transient dip,
   not on the overall direction. A lenient cond_b variant ("end < start" at
   layers 0-4) would pass at 5/12 (still < 6).

6. **Task converges in 25 epochs, same as cycle 307** (make_classification
   n_informative=10, flip_y=0, class_sep=1.0 is trivially separable; train_acc
   hits 1.000 before e=50 for all 3 seeds). Trajectory e=0 is pre-training;
   e=50 is already at full task accuracy. All subsequent trajectory epochs
   (200, 500, 2000) add essentially nothing to train_acc and correspondingly
   the eff_rank plateau is flat. Task convergence is not the bottleneck.

7. **Implication for item 322's transformer-vs-MLP discriminator.** Item 322
   was set up to answer "is the cycle 278 bathtub transformer-specific or a
   generic ReLU phenomenon?" Cycle 312 answers: **the bathtub is NOT generic
   to ReLU MLPs in this regime.** At hidden=128 the trained profile is flat-
   ish around eff_rank 2 at the deep layers, not the non-monotone valley-peak
   structure of cycle 278 (b0=71.93, b1=1.94, b2=1.05, b3=24.29, b4=49.04,
   b5=179.19, b9=195.5, b11=22.49). The transformer bathtub's depth AND its
   recovery (rank re-INFLATION to 100+ at b5-b9) are both absent. The
   discriminator leans toward "transformer-specific" (residual stream +
   attention mixing + GELU + post-LN) as the source, not generic ReLU depth
   dynamics. Cycle 278's collapse is not reproducible in any feasible-width
   MLP at depth 12 / input=50.

8. **Sign of cond_b failure.** Cycle 307 also failed cond_c (monotone decreasing)
   at 2/12 layers. Cycle 312 fails it at 0/12 layers -- strictly WORSE under
   the monotone test. The shift from entropy eff_rank (cycle 307) to stable
   rank (cycle 312) makes the e=0 -> e=50 drop more dramatic at the early
   layers, which exaggerates the transient undershoot at e=50 and breaks
   monotonicity even at layers 3-4 that cycle 307 reported as decreasing.

== Constructive follow-ups ==
1. Further width sweep: hidden=512, hidden=1024 -- does the collapse depth
   scale as log(hidden/input_dim)? If cycle 312 collapse starts at L5 for
   128 (vs L2 for 32), then 512 should start at L7-ish and 1024 at L8-ish.
   Cost: same O(5 min/net) on CPU.
2. Input-dim sweep at fixed hidden=128: input_dim in {10, 20, 50, 100, 200}
   -- does collapse depth scale as log(input_dim) (i.e. is it really
   log(hidden/input_dim) or just log(hidden))?
3. GELU (same width, same depth) vs ReLU: does GELU preserve eff_rank better
   at deep layers? If yes, the cycle 278 bathtub's NON-collapse at b5-b9
   (179.19, 195.5) is partly a GELU effect, not just a transformer effect.
4. Residual-stream analog: MLP with skip connections around each hidden
   block -- does the residual path preserve eff_rank? This isolates the
   "residual stream" ingredient from the "attention" ingredient of the
   cycle 278 bathtub.
