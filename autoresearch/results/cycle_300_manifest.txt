== Substantive findings ==
1. Item 310's hypothesis is PARTIALLY RIGHT, PARTIALLY WRONG. Direction 0 is
   the unigram/stopword axis (matches cycles 217/235) -- 17/20 stopwords.
   Direction 1 is ALSO a unigram/stopword axis -- 19/20, even more
   concentrated. The "direction 1 is semantically structured" sub-claim is
   REFUTED by this experiment.

2. The rank-2 c_proj-1 bottleneck encodes TWO different projections of the
   unigram-frequency cluster, not "unigram + semantics". The reason block-1
   eff_rank is ~2 (cycle 278) is that the unigram axis is 2-dimensional (or
   near-2D) on the block-1 output side -- not 1D.

3. Mostly-negative / GELU-bump channels dominate the INPUT side of both
   directions: 15/20 and 11/20 mostly_neg, 0/20 and 0/20 mostly_pos. The
   rank-2 bottleneck reads almost exclusively from dead / bump channels.
   This is geometrically consistent with cycles 258/278/280 -- the
   curvature region of GELU is where block-1 compressibility lives.

4. Only 12/3072 channels at block 1 are "mostly_pos" (sigma >= 0.9) at all,
   and NONE of them appear in either direction's top-20. Hypothesis C from
   item 310 (mostly-negative dominance) CONFIRMED on both directions.

5. Retrain vs SVD init: boost-token overlap 17/20 and 19/20. Adam barely
   moves the token-level output structure; the 8.6 PPL gain is happening
   WITHIN the unigram plane (row-space rotation per cycle 299) plus bias.
   A closed-form SVD-only compression would have the SAME top-20 boost
   tokens as the retrained rank-2 -- the semantic content is set by Vh[:2].

6. Block-2 K alignment is the strongest downstream signal: dir 0 top1 cos
   0.24 and dir 1 top1 cos 0.21, both >=12 sigma above null. Block-2
   attention reads the unigram axis primarily through its KEY subspace.
   Q alignment is moderate (top1 0.10 and 0.16), V alignment is weak for
   dir 0 (top1 0.14) and absent for dir 1 (top1 0.06, below null).
   Interpretation: block-1 c_proj rank-2 writes the unigram axis; block-2
   attention keys read it back; block-2 values route other information.

7. The 2D unigram bottleneck at block 1 is the geometric realisation of
   what cycle 217 called "Δ_0 rank-1 = unigram-frequency axis": at block 0
   the centred Δ was ~1D unigram; at block 1 the c_proj rank-2 encodes a
   2D refinement of the same cluster. Cycles 217/218/235 all pointed at
   this structure; this cycle verifies it at the cycle-280 A-factor level.
