== Substantive findings sharper than REFUTED ==

1. **9 / 10 pairs compose non-destructively at the +5 PPL bar.** Only
   (b10, b11) is destructive at the cycle-282-family rank anchors. The
   cycle 305 5-stack destruction CANNOT be reduced to a single bad pair --
   it must arise from a higher-order (>=3-block) interaction OR (b10, b11)
   is the trigger and other 3-/4-block tuples involving (b10, b11) inherit
   destruction. Cycle 320 verdict thus formally rejects "destructive
   composition is pervasive" and ENDORSES the cycle 305 destruction as a
   higher-order interaction.

2. **The (b10, b11) pair is the unique pair-level destruction trigger** at
   delta = +6.25 PPL. b10 r=5 and b11 r=22 are the two latest-layer
   concentrators in the cycle-282 family. They are also the two blocks
   whose pure-curvature k_95 values from cycle 304 sit at extreme positions
   (b10 mid-range, b11 = 1 = pure rank-1 terminator). Pairing the two LATE
   concentrators is destructive while pairing either of them with any
   EARLIER block (1, 2, or 3) is not. This is consistent with cycle 304's
   reading that b11 is the unique true rank-2 terminator and that touching
   b11 in the late residual stream is the most fragile move.

3. **(b1, b11) is the BEST pair** at delta = -0.8875 (best_mean 53.58
   beats the worst single anchor b11 alone at 54.47). This is the cycle 284
   CONFIRMED outcome at a halved budget; the budget reduction does not flip
   the sign. Pairing b11 with EARLY blocks (b1, b2, b3) all give delta in
   [-0.89, +0.43], i.e. effectively non-destructive even though one of them
   crosses 0. Pairing b11 with the late b10 jumps to +6.25 -- a clean
   late-vs-late vs late-vs-early dichotomy.

4. **(b1, b2) is the second-largest pair delta** at +4.87 PPL, just under
   the +5 bar. Both are r=2 concentrators in the EARLY residual stream and
   are the only pair that has the smallest combined trainable budget
   (4,608 params total). The narrow miss vs the bar suggests an extra
   measurement at higher seed count or longer N_STEPS could push this pair
   over +5; the 1-seed verdict at 2000 steps places it just under the
   threshold. Auditor flag: this pair is the most marginal and any
   follow-up should re-test it at 3 seeds and 4000 steps.

5. **The verdict is robust to budget extension on the 9 sub-threshold
   pairs.** Cycle 310 uses N_STEPS=2000; cycle 284's 4000-step (1,11)
   anchor was 52.88 vs cycle 310's 53.58 (+0.70 gap). If every pair gains
   an additional ~0.7-1 PPL with double the budget, only (1, 2) at +4.87
   could plausibly cross +5 -- and (10, 11)'s +6.25 would not move below
   +5. The REFUTED outcome would survive a 4000-step rerun on at most 9
   pairs; the (1, 2) pair is the only one that could flip status.

6. **Implication for cycle 305 destruction mechanism.** The cycle 305
   5-stack at +18.5 PPL above worst-single is NOT well-explained by
   summing pair deltas. Sum of all 10 pair deltas_v_worst = +14.96 PPL;
   sum of pair deltas excluding (10, 11) = +8.71 PPL. Even a generous
   pair-additive model would predict a 5-stack delta of order ~5-15 PPL,
   not the measured +18.5. The 5-stack must include a destructive
   triple/quadruple interaction beyond the pair level. The constructive
   follow-up would be: (a) all C(5, 3) = 10 triples to find the smallest
   tuple that reproduces destruction; (b) explicit (b10, b11, +X) triples
   to test whether (10, 11) is the seed of the higher-order destruction.

7. **The cycle 304 k_95 prediction is partially validated.** Cycle 304
   audit explicitly predicted cycle 315 destructive composition based on
   "only b11 is true rank-2 terminator". Cycle 305 confirmed at +18 PPL.
   Cycle 310 now refines: at the pair level, the prediction is wrong --
   8/9 sub-(10,11) pairs are non-destructive even though only b11 is a
   true rank-2 terminator. The destruction is not inherent to "touching
   b11 in low rank"; it is inherent to "touching BOTH late concentrators
   simultaneously". Cycle 304's prediction was correct in sign for the
   5-stack but is too pessimistic for the pair level.
