== Substantive findings ==
1. Input-dim hypothesis HOLDS. Hidden mean eff_rank 3.32 ≈ input_dim 3.
   The 4-layer MLP funnels through eff_rank-5 → 3 → 2, with the last hidden
   layer collapsing to the 2D surface dim. This is the direct geometric
   reading the item asked for.

2. Surface-dim hypothesis NEAR-HOLDS. Gradient ensemble eff_rank 2.62 is
   slightly above the predicted [1.8, 2.5] band but the discrete rank_95 = 3.
   The gradient field is dominated by 1 large normal direction plus 2 smaller
   tangential/radial directions. "≈ 2" is the right intuition near the surface;
   the 0.62 excess comes from volumetric points where the gradient also tracks
   the radial direction.

3. LASER recipe REFUTED at l* = LAST HIDDEN LAYER for SDF regression.
   Rank-3 retrain at 2.018× float crosses the verbatim REFUTED threshold (≥ 2.0)
   by 0.018. Even rank-10 retrain only reaches 1.30× float, all four fits
   BUDGET-BOUNDED at step 500. The substantive question this raises is whether
   the layer-position effect (last layer vs middle layer) is the dominant
   variable: cycle 296 spirals had l* in the middle of the stack (layers 4/5/6
   of 12) and recovered to within 0.6% of float at rank-2 with the SAME 500-step
   budget. Here l* = 3 of 4 (the last hidden), and there is no downstream layer
   to absorb the rank-truncation residual.

4. The verdict is the literal verbatim threshold result. The geometric content
   (input-dim hypothesis HOLDS, surface-dim hypothesis NEAR-HOLDS, last-hidden
   layer compression is hard at this budget) is sharper than REFUTED conveys
   on its own.
