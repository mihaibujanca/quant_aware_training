== Substantive findings ==

1. **REFUTED at the verbatim 1/10 pair-destruction count.** 9 / 10 pairs
   compose non-destructively (delta_vs_worst_single in [-0.89, +4.87]).
   Only (b10, b11) crosses the +5 PPL bar at +6.25. The "destructive
   composition is pervasive at the pair level" hypothesis is rejected.

2. **The (b10, b11) pair is the unique pair-level destruction trigger.**
   b10 and b11 are the two LATEST cycle-282 concentrators in the residual
   stream. Pairing the two late blocks is destructive while pairing either
   with any earlier block (1, 2, or 3) is not. This is consistent with
   cycle 304's reading that b11 is the unique true rank-2 terminator and
   that touching b11 in the late residual stream is the most fragile move,
   but it refines the prediction: the fragility manifests when b11 is
   paired with the OTHER late concentrator b10, not b11 alone.

3. **(b1, b11) is the BEST pair** at delta = -0.8875 (best_mean 53.58 BEATS
   the worst single anchor b11 = 54.47). This is the cycle 284 CONFIRMED
   outcome reproduced at a halved budget; the budget reduction does not
   flip the sign. Pairing b11 with EARLY blocks (1, 2, 3) gives delta in
   [-0.89, +0.43], i.e. effectively non-destructive even when the delta
   crosses zero. The early/late dichotomy is sharp.

4. **(b1, b2) is the second-largest pair delta** at +4.87 PPL, just under
   the +5 bar. Both are r=2 EARLY concentrators with the smallest
   combined trainable budget (4,608 params). The narrow miss vs the bar
   suggests the 1-seed 2000-step measurement might flip if extended; an
   audit re-test at 3 seeds and 4000 steps could push it over, but a
   single pair flipping CONFIRMED would still leave verdict at 2/10
   (REFUTED bar holds at <=2).

5. **Cycle 305's +18 PPL destruction is NOT a sum-of-pair-deltas effect.**
   Sum of pair deltas_v_worst across all 10 pairs = +14.96 PPL. Even an
   "additive over all C(5,2) pairs" model would predict ~5-15 PPL of
   composition penalty for the 5-stack, far below the measured +18.5.
   The 5-stack must include a destructive triple/quadruple interaction
   beyond the pair level.

6. **Constructive follow-up.** Two natural P0 candidates emerge: (a)
   C(5, 3) = 10 triples to find the smallest tuple that reproduces
   destruction, especially triples that include (10, 11); (b) sequential
   coordinate-descent over blocks to test whether the joint init basin
   is the issue (cycle 305 audit suggested this).

7. **Cycle 304 k_95 prediction is partially validated.** Cycle 304 predicted
   cycle 315 destruction; cycle 305 confirmed at +18 PPL. Cycle 310 now
   refines: at the pair level, the prediction is too pessimistic --
   non-(10,11) pairs all compose. The k_95 = 1 b11 termination is a
   trigger for late-late destruction, not a universal "touching b11 in
   low rank breaks composition".
