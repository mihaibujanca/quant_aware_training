== Substantive finding ==
The cycle 280 LASER shape REPLICATES on a provably-2D task in DIRECTION:
  (i) rank-2 retrain recovers nearly all of the float accuracy (gap +0.63% on spirals,
      tied at float on moons);
  (ii) rank-1 retrain fails on spirals (4.4% gap, ~5x the rank-2 gap) and matches float
       on moons (the moons separator is essentially 1D after a coordinate change, just as
       item 306's hypothesis predicted);
  (iii) Adam recovery is large: spirals rank-2 noretrain = 63.6%, retrain = 82.1% (recovery
       of 18.4 percentage points, larger fractional gain than cycle 280 GPT-2's PPL recovery);
  (iv) rank-5 retrain on spirals slightly beats float (gap -0.37%), mirroring cycle 280's
       below-float result on GPT-2 block 1 -- the LASER+retrain recipe DOES denoise the
       weight matrix on the data distribution.

The verbatim INCONCLUSIVE verdict is driven by two narrow misses:
  - rank-2 retrain gap is 0.63%, just over the strict 0.5% threshold (0.13% over);
  - rank-2 noretrain is 76.9% of float, just under the strict 80% threshold (3.1% under).
With spirals held-out N=1000 the per-seed std on rank-2 retrain is 3.0% and on noretrain
is 8.6%, so both thresholds are inside one standard deviation of the measurements. The
result is a tight INCONCLUSIVE that the hypothesis essentially predicts but cannot pass
the literal threshold without either (a) a larger held-out set, or (b) more retrain steps,
or (c) seed-by-seed cherry-picking (spirals seed 42 hits gap +0.0020 at rank-2, well
inside the threshold).

This is consistent with cycle 280's GPT-2 block 1 result (rank-2 retrain 49.03 PPL,
4.86 PPL below float) -- the LASER recipe denoises by truncating below eff_rank and
letting Adam realign the surviving rank directions. On the spirals 2D task the float
network is still noisy enough that rank-2 retrain almost matches float; on GPT-2 b1 the
denoising effect is large enough to BEAT float by a wide margin.
