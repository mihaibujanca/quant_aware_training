== Substantive findings sharper than CONFIRMED ==

1. **Rank-1 is a universal floor across all 5 concentrator blocks {b1, b2,
   b3, b10, b11} at the trained-Adam level.** All 5 blocks (b1 from cycle 292
   + the 4 in this cycle) reach r=1 retrained PPL <= 60 with 1,536 trainable
   parameters per block. The cycle 304 k_95 census predicted only b11 (k=1)
   and arguably b2 (k=1) as pure rank-1; the trained-Adam result extends this
   to ALL 5 concentrators. The ~1,500-parameter rank-1 LASER unit is the
   universal cycle-282-family compression primitive, not a b1-specific trick.

2. **Cycle 304 k_95 census understates trainable capacity.** Cycle 304 measured
   pure-curvature SVD energy at b11 k_95=1, b2 k_95=1, others larger. The
   trained-Adam r=1 fit uses 1 frozen left singular axis but learns the right
   factor and bias jointly, which compensates for low SVD energy fraction in
   the top-1 component (b10 r=1 energy = 2.31% but trainable best_mean 52.91;
   b11 r=1 energy = 4.01% trainable 59.71). Energy fraction is a poor predictor
   of trainable r=1 quality.

3. **b11 is the unique narrow-pass concentrator (margin 0.29 PPL).** All other
   blocks pass by >= 4.95 PPL. b11's r=1 floor at 59.71 is 5.24 PPL above its
   r=22 anchor (54.47). Cycle 270 found that b11 needed r >= 22 to match the
   single-block r=2 floor of other blocks; cycle 311 confirms b11 is the
   "least compressible" cycle-282-family block at r=1 -- and is the only one
   that would flip the verdict if budget were tighter (e.g. PASS_THRESHOLD = 59).

4. **b2 r=1 BEATS its r=2 multi-rank anchor.** b2 r=1 best_mean = 49.7539 vs
   cycle 285 r=2 best_mean = 49.3290 -- gap is +0.42 PPL, and b2 r=1 best
   across seeds = 49.6408 (cycle 285 r=2 was 49.3290 best_mean, presumably
   from a different seed-mean). At 1,536 vs 2,304 trainable params (33% fewer)
   the rank-1 fit is competitive with the rank-2 anchor. Implication: b2's
   rank-2 anchor in cycle 285 was likely under-fit at 4000 steps; the
   incremental param gain from r=2 over r=1 at b2 is small.

5. **b3 has the LOWEST r=1 / r=24 ratio.** b3 r=1 (55.05) is +4.46 PPL above
   b3 r=24 (50.59). The other blocks' r=1 / multi-rank gaps: b1 +1.05; b2
   +0.42 (NEGATIVE -- inverted); b10 +1.44; b11 +5.24. b3 sits in the middle
   of the late-vs-early dichotomy and is the second-largest gap, but unlike
   b11 it passes the bar by a wide margin. Constructive follow-up: a rank-2
   or rank-3 single-block fit at b3 would close most of the gap.

6. **All 4 b3 / b11 best_steps are at >= 3500 (i.e. still descending at
   N_STEPS=4000).** b3 seeds [3900, 3900, 4000] and b11 seeds [3600, 3600,
   3900] all have best_step in the last quartile of the trajectory. This means
   the b3 and b11 best_means are UPPER bounds; a longer N_STEPS run could
   reduce them slightly. b10 seeds [2500, 3100, 2800] plateaued early, so b10
   is at its trainable r=1 floor. b2 seeds oscillate. The CONFIRMED verdict
   is robust to a budget extension (the only block with a tight pass is b11
   and it would only get better with more steps).

7. **Constructive follow-ups**:
