== Geometric object ==

Uncentered variant (cycle 280 recipe):
  SVD(W) = U S Vh
  A_unc = U[:, :2] * diag(S[:2])  in R^{3072 x 2}   (frozen)
  B_unc = Vh[:2, :]                in R^{2 x 768}   (trainable)
  bias_unc                         in R^{768}       (trainable)
  forward: x @ (A_unc @ B_unc) + bias_unc

Calibrated variant (cycle 313 recipe + SVD split):
  hook X (25600, 3072) on 200 calib x 128 tok
  Sigma = X^T X / 25600
  A_l = Sigma^(1/2) via symmetric eigh
  SVD(A_l @ W) = U_w S_w Vh_w
  W_r = A_l_pinv @ (U_w[:, :2] @ diag(S_w[:2]) @ Vh_w[:2, :])
  SVD(W_r) = U_r S_r Vh_r
  A_cal = U_r[:, :2] * diag(S_r[:2])  in R^{3072 x 2}  (frozen)
  B_cal = Vh_r[:2, :]                 in R^{2 x 768}   (trainable)
  bias_cal                            in R^{768}       (trainable)
  forward: x @ (A_cal @ B_cal) + bias_cal

The two frozen A factors span different rank-2 subspaces of R^{3072}
(span(U[:,:2]) vs span(U_r[:,:2])). A_cal is oriented to minimize
||X(W - W_r)||_F under the empirical input distribution (SVD-LLM
optimality at no-retrain), which gives the 56.70 vs 57.66 init gap.
After Adam retraining of B and bias, both variants converge to
~49.0 PPL: the 768-dim B+bias space has enough capacity to "rotate
within" the 2D column space and reach the output-space optimum
regardless of which specific 2D slice is pinned by A.

At b1 r=2, the rank-2 output column space constraint is NOT the
binding constraint -- the binding constraint is the weight-space
direction that Adam fits within the constrained column space, which
is the same for both init variants modulo the absorbed basis
rotation. The 8.63 PPL retrain gap is the "width" of that
constrained descent, not of the column space choice.
