== Substantive findings sharper than CONFIRMED ==

1. **(b10, b11) r=1 WORSENS vs cycle 310.** Cycle 310 heterogeneous r_10=5
   r_11=22 (55 trainable rank units, ~22K params) -> best_mean 60.7161
   (+6.2474 above worst single). Cycle 314 r=1+r=1 (3,072 params, ~7.3x
   smaller) -> best_mean 70.0146 (+10.3072). The delta vs cycle 310 is
   +9.30 PPL -- reducing capacity MAKES THE DESTRUCTION WORSE. This is
   the strongest possible signal that the (b10, b11) pair cannot share a
   low-dimensional c_proj subspace regardless of basis; the two late
   residual-stream concentrators write into structurally-conflicting
   output directions. Rank-1 removes the extra subspace degrees of
   freedom that allowed partial decoupling at r_10=5 r_11=22.

2. **(b1, b2) is a NEW destructive cell at r=1** (+5.9144, not seen in
   cycle 310's +4.8739 marginal miss). The early-early pair was
   marginal at heterogeneous ranks; the rank-1 reduction tips it over
   the bar. delta vs cycle 310 = +1.79 PPL. (b1, b2) is the second
   cycle 314 destructive cell. Notable: unlike (b10, b11), the
   (b1, b2) r=1 destruction is only +1.79 PPL worse than heterogeneous,
   suggesting a much softer mechanism -- it may go away at 3 seeds /
   4000 steps (cycle 310's marginal miss + cycle 314's marginal hit at
   2000 steps 1 seed is noise-bounded).

3. **(b2, b11) is now the BEST pair** at delta -1.4656 (beats worst
   single b11 r=1 = 59.71 by 1.47 PPL). Cycle 310's best pair was
   (b1, b11) at -0.8875. At r=1, (b2, b11) -1.47 > (b1, b11) -0.85:
   both EARLY-b11 pairs are non-destructive at r=1, and pairing with
   b2 gives a stronger "rising tide" than pairing with b1. The worst

== Geometric object ==

For each pair (b_i, b_j) in C(5, 2) = 10 pairs:
  A_i = U_i[:, :1] * S_i[:1]  in R^{3072 x 1}  (frozen buffer)
  B_i in R^{1 x 768}          (trainable, init = Vh_i[:1, :])
  bias_i in R^{768}           (trainable, init = W_proj_i.bias)
  [same for block j]

The rank-1 approximation W_i_rank1 = A_i @ B_i = (U_i[:,:1]*S_i[:1]) @ Vh_i[:1,:]
replaces c_proj_i.weight; rank-r SVD truncation at r=1 as a starting point,
then B and bias are trained jointly for both blocks simultaneously. The
frozen A_i pins the LEFT singular direction but lets the right factor
and bias drift during Adam.

At rank-1 each block contributes a single c_proj output column direction
(the single frozen left singular vector of W_proj_i). The (b10, b11) pair
puts two such directions into the residual stream; at rank-1 the pair
has NO degrees of freedom to decouple them, and Adam cannot re-orient
A_10 or A_11. The destruction at (b10, b11) measures exactly this: the
two frozen directions are structurally incompatible at the residual
stream level.

At heterogeneous rank (cycle 310 r_10=5 r_11=22) the pair had 27 frozen
left singular directions across the two blocks, giving Adam a 27-D
subspace to fit the pair jointly. The 60.72 PPL at cycle 310 reflected
this partial decoupling; at rank-1 the decoupling disappears entirely
and PPL jumps to 70.01 (+9.30 worse).
