== Substantive reading ==

1. REFUTED branch a: Adam finds basin-independent ~49.0 PPL from either
   top-2 SVD basis or top-2 whitened SVD basis. The 768-dim trainable
   B + bias space absorbs the basis rotation between init variants
   during retraining. Init choice is load-irrelevant at retrain time
   for b1 r=2.

2. Calibrated retrain IS marginally better at every seed (-0.0748,
   -0.0423, -0.1156 PPL; mean -0.078). Directional consistency means the
   effect is real, but magnitude is ~12x smaller than the init gap and
   well inside the per-variant cross-seed std (0.025 - 0.050). For
   verdict-bearing purposes, this is noise-level.

3. Cycle 313's "calibration helps at 74/84 cells" finding is
   no-retrain-only. It does NOT translate to a measurable improvement
   at the retrain ceiling at b1 r=2. Calibrated SVD-LLM is valuable
   for zero-retrain compression (56.70 < 57.66) and useless (within
   0.08 PPL noise) for retrain-based compression.

4. The 8.63 PPL retrain gap (cycle 280's 57.66 init -> 49.03 retrain)
   is basis-independent at b1 r=2. Adam is fitting a non-SVD-orthogonal
   direction in the 768-dim B space that is unreachable from either
   init by construction (the frozen A constrains the reachable 2D output
   column space; the retrain finds the optimal B for the constrained
   column space). Both init variants have the same 2D constrained
   column space (within a rotation that Adam can undo via B), so both
   converge to the same retrain optimum.
