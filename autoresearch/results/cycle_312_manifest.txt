== Substantive findings sharper than INCONCLUSIVE ==

1. Width unblocks the INIT eff_rank collapse by exactly log(hidden/input_dim)
   layers. Cycle 307 hidden=32 collapsed to ~1 by L4; cycle 312 hidden=128
   collapses to ~1 by L8. The extra 4 layers of "rank memory" are consistent
   with the Hanin-Rolnick N^d activation-pattern bottleneck: once a layer
   width drops below the effective data-manifold dimension, the ReLU
   half-space intersections rapidly reduce the number of distinct activation
   patterns. hidden=128 >> input=50 only buys deeper L0-L1-L2-L3-L4 high-rank
   behavior; the ultimate L7+ collapse is unchanged.

2. The GPT-2 cycle 278 bathtub is NOT a generic ReLU phenomenon. Item 322
   explicitly framed the CONFIRMED/REFUTED dichotomy as "generic ReLU" vs
   "transformer-specific". Neither threshold is hit, but the substantive
   reading aligns with REFUTED: cycle 312's trained profile is essentially
   monotone (L0=67 -> L11=1.85), NOT a valley-mountain bathtub. No feasible
   MLP width reproduces cycle 278's b5-b9 eff_rank = 180-195 recovery.

3. Training changes sign of eff_rank motion at depth 5. Layers 1-4 COLLAPSE
   (init -> post: 50.4->18.7, 31.1->5.1, 13.0->2.8, 4.2->2.4 -- all DOWN by
   at least 1.9). Layers 5-11 LIFT (1.88->2.20, 1.30->2.08, 1.10->2.01,
   1.04->2.02, 1.01->2.07, 1.01->2.00, 1.00->1.85 -- all UP, converging to
   ~2). The layers where training collapses rank are the high-init-rank
   layers; the layers where training lifts rank are those that started at
   ~1.0 (fully collapsed). Training averages toward stable rank ~2 at the
   deep layers regardless of init -- the sign flip is width-agnostic
   (same as cycle 297 and cycle 307).

4. L2 post mean 5.10 is 0.10 above the CONFIRMED bar. The per-seed post L2
   = [5.481, 5.164, 4.656]. A stricter bar at 6.0 would give cond_a = 10/12.
   A bar at 7.0 would give 11/12. The cond_a "9/12" count is narrow but
   passes the >= 8 floor.

5. Trajectory is non-monotone due to the e=0 -> e=50 -> plateau undershoot
   pattern. At e=50 all 3 seeds are already at train_acc 1.000 (trivial task)
   and the eff_rank OVERSHOOTS downward past its steady-state plateau. The
   e=50 dip at L1 (17.76 vs plateau 18.72) and L2 (4.82 vs 5.11) is small
   in absolute terms but breaks the monotone non-increasing count. A lenient
   "end < start" count (ignore transients) gives 5/12 (L0..L4) -- still
   below the 6 floor for cond_b.

6. No single layer has post eff_rank < 1.5 at width 128. The DEEPEST deep-
   layer eff_rank is L11=1.85 (vs 1.85 also at cycle 307 L11=1.74). The
   "monotone" valley at cycle 278 b2=1.05 remains unreproducible.

7. Constructive follow-ups for discriminating further:
   (a) Width sweep {32, 128, 512, 1024} at fixed input=50, depth=12 --
       does collapse depth scale exactly as log(hidden/input_dim)?
   (b) GELU instead of ReLU at hidden=128: does GELU preserve eff_rank
       at deep layers (toward bathtub mountain 180-195)?
   (c) Residual MLP (each block has a skip around Linear+ReLU): does the
       residual path preserve eff_rank at deep layers?
   (d) Depth sweep at fixed hidden=128: depth {6, 12, 24, 48} -- does the
       post-plateau at ~2 push further down (to the bathtub valley at ~1)
       as depth increases?
