Reading: the literal "rank-22 weight SVD pays for itself" hypothesis is
mostly WRONG (no-retrain costs +5.79 PPL, far above the ≤1 PPL the brief
predicted). The "rank-22 subspace + Adam retrain" hypothesis is correct by
a hair (54.469 < 54.5 by 0.031). The headline geometric finding is that
weight SVD ≠ activation SVD: cycle 267's eff_rank=22.49 was measured on the
OUTPUT activations (in R^768), but the WEIGHT matrix (3072 → 768) is
full-rank and well-conditioned (top-100 SVs only carry 34.83% of energy).
The 22-dim activation subspace is created by the JOINT action of c_fc and
c_proj on a specific input distribution, NOT by the top-22 right singular
vectors of c_proj alone. Adam recovers -5.22 PPL with 17K params to land at
54.469, just 0.574 above float — the (17,664, 54.47) point opens a new
low-param row of the Pareto frontier between cycle 269 (9K, 91.61) and
cycle 268 (200K, 55.95), and dominates cycle 268 on PPL at 8.7% of the
trainable budget.
