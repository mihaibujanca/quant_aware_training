== Substantive findings sharper than REFUTED ==

1. **Adam erases the init basis difference.** Uncentered init 57.66 and
   calibrated init 56.70 differ by +0.96 PPL (12% of the retrain gap),
   but after 4000 Adam steps the two best_means converge to within 0.08
   PPL (49.0324 vs 48.9549, delta -0.0775). The calibrated advantage is
   reduced by a factor of ~12x during retraining. The 8.63 PPL retrain
   gap from cycle 280's init (57.66 -> 49.03) is basis-independent: it
   closes equally well from either SVD-LLM or uncentered starting point.

2. **Directional consistency -- calibrated is very slightly better.**
   All 3 seed pairs show calibrated < uncentered at convergence:
     seed 42:   49.0603 -> 48.9855 (delta -0.0748)
     seed 137:  49.0367 -> 48.9944 (delta -0.0423)
     seed 2024: 49.0004 -> 48.8848 (delta -0.1156)
   Mean per-seed delta = -0.0776 PPL. The calibrated retrain is
   consistently but not substantially below uncentered. The 0.08 PPL
   improvement is within the cross-seed std of the uncentered variant
   (0.0246), i.e. it does not exceed seed-level noise; all three
   individual deltas are also within 1-sigma of the std bar. REFUTED
   per the verbatim 0.3 PPL noise bar.

3. **Uncentered retrain reproduces cycle 280 exactly.** Cycle 280 b1 r=2
   reported best_mean = 49.03; cycle 315 uncentered = 49.0324 (delta
   +0.0024 PPL, far under the 0.1 PPL tolerance). The cycle 280 retrain
   loop is reproducible to 0.003 PPL across 3 seeds x 4000 steps on mps.

4. **b1 basin is basin-independent but shared.** Adam finds a basin at
   ~49.0 PPL regardless of whether it starts from the top-2 SVD basis
   or the top-2 whitened SVD basis. The two init points are
   geometrically distinct (at r=2 the frozen A matrix differs by
   non-trivial rotation), yet both converge to the same final PPL.
   This is consistent with the 768-dim trainable B + bias space having
   enough capacity to absorb the basis rotation during retraining.

5. **cycle 313 calibration-helps result is no-retrain-only.** Cycle
   313's per-block calibrated vs uncentered mean delta -1.32 at b1
   (averaged over r=1..64) reflects a genuine improvement at no-
   retrain; it does NOT translate to a measurable improvement at
   the retrain ceiling. The 12% of the gap closed by calibration at
   init is absorbed by Adam, not amplified.

6. **This result kills the "calibrated init improves compressibility"
   branch at b1 r=2.** Any future retrain-based LASER compression
   work should use whichever init is cheaper to compute (uncentered
   SVD has no calibration overhead) because the init basis does not
   affect the trained-Adam floor at this rank/block. The SVD-LLM
   method's value is in no-retrain-only settings (PPL 56.70 vs 57.66
   at r=2 matters when retraining is forbidden).
