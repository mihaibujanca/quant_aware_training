Cycle 021 — Grokking as Compression Catalyst: Toy ReLU
======================================================

Item: 39
Verdict: REFUTED

Key findings:
- Classic grokking IS present on spirals with n_train=200, WD=0.001:
  train accuracy saturates at 92% by epoch 2000, test accuracy jumps
  from 0.747 → 0.805+ around epoch 17000 (a 5.8 pp rise = 58% of the
  train→test gap closed).
- The "partition collapses during grokking" hypothesis is WRONG for spirals:
  pattern count INCREASES through grokking (369 → 564 at grokking epoch,
  then up to 723 peak). No downward phase transition observed.
- Pattern drop from peak-to-post: 4.3% (criterion: ≥40% — not met).
- Compression accuracy improvement (pre vs post grokking, 4× distillation):
  +5.7% (0.602 → 0.659, criterion: ≥10% — not met).
- The compression gain tracks teacher accuracy improvement (+5.8 pp test acc),
  not geometric simplification.
- Weight norms are stable (28.7 → 34.0 → 32.5) — the large-norm spike
  associated with grokking in modular arithmetic is mild here.
- Single-affine residual is consistently ~0.94-0.97 throughout training,
  confirming the function remains highly nonlinear before and after grokking.

Mechanistic explanation:
  On continuous 2D tasks, grokking = the network finds a better-fitting
  nonlinear solution (more linear regions, not fewer). On discrete algebraic
  tasks (modular arithmetic), grokking = the network finds a compact
  algorithmic solution (fewer regions). The compression catalyst hypothesis
  is domain-specific to discrete tasks.
