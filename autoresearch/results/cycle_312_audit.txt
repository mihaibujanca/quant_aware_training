== Substantive reading — auditor endorses ==

1. Width x4 (32->128) pushes the ReLU init eff_rank collapse ~4 layers
   deeper (cycle 307 ~1 by L4, cycle 312 ~1 by L8). The shift is
   consistent with log(hidden/input_dim) scaling of the Hanin-Rolnick
   activation-pattern bottleneck. Width helps but only logarithmically;
   the deep-layer collapse is unchanged.

2. Training COLLAPSES eff_rank at layers 1-4 (L1 50.4->18.7, L2
   31.1->5.1, L3 13.0->2.8, L4 4.2->2.4) and LIFTS eff_rank at layers
   5-11 (L5 1.88->2.20, ..., L11 1.00->1.85). Sign flip at depth 5,
   same as cycles 297 and 307 (both hidden=32). Width-agnostic.
   Trained profile converges to ~2 at all deep layers.

3. Cycle 278 GPT-2 bathtub (b1=1.94, b2=1.05, b5=179, b9=195) is NOT
   reproduced at any feasible MLP width. Cycle 312's post profile is
   monotone decreasing from 67 (L0) to ~2 (L3+), NOT the valley-
   mountain structure. Neither the valley depth recovery NOR the
   mountain at intermediate blocks is present. The substantive reading
   aligns with REFUTED even though the REFUTED bar (init >= 60 all L
   AND post >= 30 all L) is missed wholesale -- the MLP cannot produce
   the bathtub in either direction. The discriminator leans toward
   "transformer-specific" (residual stream + attention mixing + GELU +
   post-LN) as the origin of the GPT-2 bathtub.

4. cond_b fails on trajectory artifacts, not on overall direction.
   Layers 0-4 undershoot the plateau at e=50 (e.g. L2 e=50=4.82,
   plateau 5.11, dip 0.29) then rebound; layers 5-11 are strictly UP
   (wrong sign for non-increasing). A "first vs last" alternative
   metric counts 5 layers net-down (L0..L4, 5/12), still below the
   >=6 floor. Layers 5-11 (7/12) are strictly UP and cannot be
   salvaged for monotone-decreasing under any definition.

5. L2 post mean 5.10 is a narrow miss by 0.10. Per-seed post L2 =
   [5.481, 5.164, 4.656]. A bar at 6.0 would give cond_a = 10/12.
   The verdict is not sensitive to this narrow miss (cond_a still
   passes at 9/12 >= 8).
