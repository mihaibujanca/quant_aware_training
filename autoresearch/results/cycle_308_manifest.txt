== Substantive findings sharper than REFUTED ==

(1) Cycle 300's "unigram axes" reading does NOT lift to a constructive
    direction in R^768. Cycle 300 CONFIRMED_unigram by classifying the
    top-20 boost tokens of (W_E @ B_final[i]) as 17/20 and 19/20
    stopwords/punct -- a token-set classification of the trained B_final
    rows. Cycle 308 tests whether the underlying R^768 direction is
    RECOVERABLE from the natural model-agnostic constructions:
      d1 = (W_E^T @ log_p) / ||...||                 (unigram log-freq axis)
      d2 = (W_E^T @ s_vec) Gram-Schmidt to d1        (stopword indicator axis)
    Substituting [d1; d2] for the trained Vh-rows in the rank-2 LASER
    factorisation lands at PPL 127.5 (best of 25 grid points), 70 PPL
    worse than the no-retrain SVD init 57.66 and 78 PPL worse than the
    trained rank-2 49.03. The TOKEN-LEVEL "unigram" classification does
    NOT imply VECTOR-LEVEL "unigram axis" recovery: the trained B_final
    rows and the W_E^T @ log_p / W_E^T @ s_vec directions live in
    different parts of R^768, and the difference is geometrically
    decisive.

(2) The grid minimum lies at the lower-left corner (0.5, 0.5). Smaller
    scales would likely give lower PPL, but the brief specifies a fixed
    25-point grid {0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10}^2 with no data-fit beyond it. The
    cycle 280 SVD init has unit-norm Vh rows, so s1=s2=1 would be the
    "matched magnitude" point if d1, d2 were the right unit vectors.
    They are not: at (1, 1) PPL=577.69 (10x worse than the SVD init at
    the SAME magnitude), confirming that DIRECTION (not magnitude) is
    what cycle 308 tests.

(3) Asymmetric scale dependence. PPL is monotone-increasing in s2 at
    every fixed s1 in {0.5, 1, 2}, and roughly increasing in s1 at the
    smaller s2 values. The stopword-indicator direction d2 is the
    DOMINANT damage source: even at s2=0.5, increasing s1 from 0.5 to
    5.0 inflates PPL from 128 -> 52,486 (410x); at s2=2.0, the same s1
    sweep gives 1704 -> 14,552 (8.5x). The hand-designed d2 axis points
    in a direction that is geometrically BAD for block 1 c_proj (any
    nonzero output along it actively damages downstream computation).

(4) Comparison anchors:

      cycle 280 trained rank-2 (best)        =  49.03  (-4.86 vs float)
      cycle 280 no-retrain SVD init          =  57.66  (+3.77 vs float)

    only difference is the choice of B rows. The SVD top-2 right
    singular vectors of W_proj_1 are NOT (d1, d2) in any approximate
    sense.

(5) Hypothesis closure: cycle 300's "block 1 is a 2D unigram-frequency
    projection" has a sharper-than-REFUTED restatement. Cycle 300 is
    consistent with the BOOST-TOKEN classification being an INDIRECT
    consequence of the trained B factor's projection through the LM
    head -- many R^768 vectors will project (via W_E) onto a token list
    dominated by stopwords (because stopwords are the most frequent
    tokens and any approximately-uniform direction gets boosted toward
    them). The vector-recovery test (cycle 308) discriminates between
    "B factor is mechanistically a unigram axis" (would CONFIRM here)
    and "B factor's TOP-20 boost list happens to look unigram-like
    because of LM-head geometry" (would REFUTE here). Cycle 308 selects
    the second reading.

(6) Implication for compression: the rank-2 floor at block 1 cannot

== Geometric object ==

For block l = 1:
  W_orig in R^(3072, 768)               (mlp.c_proj.weight, HF Conv1D)
  bias in R^768                          (frozen)
  SVD: W_orig = U diag(S) Vh             (full thin SVD)
  A_frozen = U[:, :2] @ diag(S[:2]) in R^(3072, 2)   (cycle 280 LEFT factor)
  Vh_init = Vh[:2, :] in R^(2, 768)                  (cycle 280 SVD-init RIGHT)

Synthetic:
  count(t) = unigram count of token t over 25,600 calib tokens
  p(t) = count(t) / 25600
  log_p in R^50257 = log(p + 1/(N*10))
  d1_raw in R^768 = wte^T @ log_p
  d1 = d1_raw / ||d1_raw||                    ||d1_raw|| = 1,257,193.4
  s_vec in {0,1}^50257 = stopword indicator   (747 ones)
  d2_raw in R^768 = wte^T @ s_vec             ||d2_raw|| = 1,318.65
  d2 = (d2_raw - <d2_raw, d1> d1)             ||d2_orth|| = 636.55
  d2 = d2 / ||d2||
  |<d1, d2>| = 2.50e-07 after Gram-Schmidt
  B_synth(s1, s2) = stack([s1 * d1, s2 * d2]) in R^(2, 768)
  W_repl(s1, s2) = A_frozen @ B_synth(s1, s2) in R^(3072, 768)

  Replace model.transformer.h[1].mlp.c_proj.weight = W_repl
  bias unchanged. Trainable params = 0.
  Eval PPL on 100 wikitext-2 val seqs x 128 tokens.
