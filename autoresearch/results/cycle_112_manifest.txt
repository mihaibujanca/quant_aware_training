=== ROOT CAUSE ===
Hypothesis failure: The plan assumed N_pos ≈ 1000+ channels per block (based on
cycle 111's neg_frac ~73-87%). But cycle 111 measured neg_frac per-token-average,
not per-channel. In reality:
  - neg_frac[j] = fraction of TOKENS where channel j is negative
  - Threshold neg_frac < 0.5 selects channels positive in majority of tokens
  - With mean_z ≈ -1 (all blocks), nearly all channels are negative-majority
  - Blocks 4, 7, 11: only 53-71 positive channels out of 3072 (< 3%)

When k_eff = min(512, N_pos) = 53-71, compression ratio ≈ 46x (not 6x).
This destroys PPL for blocks 4, 7, 11 regardless of leak correction.

B and C are identical in PPL — confirming the b_leak correction has zero effect
when channel selection is the bottleneck. The bias is irrelevant once
the channel count is this low.

=== WHAT THIS MEANS ===
The leak-aware compression idea (cycle 105's fix) cannot be evaluated with
a hard pos/neg channel partition. A revised experiment would need to either:
  (a) Use a continuous weighting scheme (weight channels by their positive fraction)
  (b) Apply leak correction on top of standard topk (no partitioning)
  (c) Test on block 1 only where N_pos ≈ 546 and the partition is meaningful
