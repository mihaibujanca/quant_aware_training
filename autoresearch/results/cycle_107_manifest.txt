Cycle 107 — Output-Space Direct Regression Bypass — GPT-2 FFN
Item: 117

Summary
-------
Direct rank-r regression (SVD(X) basis + lstsq Y≈X@V_r@A^T+b) beats topk+lstsq
at matched parameter count in 2/4 tested blocks (blocks 4 and 7), confirming that
bypassing the GELU hidden layer entirely can be competitive with or better than
preserving the hidden-layer structure for middle transformer blocks.

Key Numbers
-----------
Baseline PPL: ~66.4 (100 eval seqs, OWT)
Blocks where direct wins: 4 (Δ=-0.50% at r=32), 7 (Δ=-0.39% to -0.41% at r=32-64)
Block 1 (early): topk strongly better — Δ=+26.8% at r=32, narrows to +2.1% at r=384
Block 11 (late): topk better — Δ=+4-6% across all ranks
Delta range: [-0.50%, +26.8%] (negative = direct wins)

Interpretation
--------------
- Middle blocks (4, 7): FFN behaves more like a simple projection; GELU structure
  provides little advantage. Direct regression is equally or slightly more efficient.
- Early block (1): hidden-layer nonlinearity provides substantial signal; bypassing
  loses information that a small k-channel GELU network preserves.
- Late block (11): similarly, hidden structure helps at r<384.
- Recon error: direct consistently lower than topk at matched r/k, but PPL diverges
  in blocks 1 and 11 — reconstruction error is a poor PPL predictor for these blocks.
