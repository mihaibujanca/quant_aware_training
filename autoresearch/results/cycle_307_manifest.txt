== Substantive findings sharper than INCONCLUSIVE ==

(1) The 50D input raises init eff_rank above the spirals 2D floor ONLY
    at layer 0 (15.70 vs spirals 2.99). Every other layer is ALREADY
    collapsed at init: L1=5.93, L2=2.96, L3=1.35, L4=1.05, L5=1.01,
    L6=1.00, L7..L11=1.000 (exact to 3 decimals). The random Gaussian
    + ReLU activation-pattern bottleneck (Hanin & Rolnick N^d with the
    binding constraint being min(input_dim, hidden_width)=32 at layer 0
    and then the SELF-collapse of layer l+1 into layer l's post-ReLU
    manifold) produces an effectively 2D-like init at depth 4+ even
    when the input is 50-dimensional. The input-dim lift is ONE LAYER
    DEEP.

(2) Training LIFTS eff_rank at layers 3-11, same sign as cycle 297
    spirals (upward trajectory). Contrary to item 317 CONFIRMED branch
    hypothesis of a "collapse to rank-2 during training", training
    REDUCES eff_rank only at the first two layers (L0: 15.7 -> 13.5;
    L1: 5.9 -> 2.4) and INCREASES it at layers 3-11 from ~1.0 up to
    ~1.5-1.7. This is the same sign as cycle 297 and OPPOSITE to the
    cycle 278 GPT-2 c_proj output bathtub (which has layers at
    eff_rank 1.94 / 1.05 and a bathtub shape).

(3) Task convergence is TRIVIAL: all 3 seeds reach train_acc = 1.0 in
    50 epochs (the first eval step after epoch 50 captures the traj;
    actual target_acc=0.90 was already met by epoch 25). The
    make_classification(n_informative=10, n_classes=2, flip_y=0) task
    is linearly separable, so the network fits it trivially and any
    training-induced rank dynamics after epoch ~50 are post-fit
    artifacts (the trajectory plateau from e=200 onwards confirms
    this).

(4) Implication for cycle 278: even with a 50D input, a 32-wide ReLU
    MLP cannot reproduce the GPT-2 bathtub qualitatively. The cycle 278
    collapse (b1=1.94, b2=1.05) cannot be attributed to "generic
    implicit regularization on overparameterized ReLU nets" via this
    higher-dim MLP testbed either. To get a genuine "training causes
    eff_rank collapse" signal, one would need EITHER a hidden width
    >> input dim (so layer-0 random-ReLU bottleneck is not the binding
    constraint) OR a non-ReLU activation (GELU, sigmoid) that does not
    pin activation patterns to orthant subsets, OR a transformer
    architecture where residual streams carry high-rank signal past
    individual FFN blocks.
