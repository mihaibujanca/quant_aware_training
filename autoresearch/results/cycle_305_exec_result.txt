== Substantive findings ==

1. Item 315 hypothesis is REFUTED at the verbatim 55 PPL bar by a margin
   of 18 PPL. The 5 single-block rank floors are INCOMPATIBLE: GPT-2 small
   cannot simultaneously give up the low-rank c_proj subspaces at all 5
   "concentrator" blocks {1, 2, 3, 10, 11}.

2. The init PPL 150.36 is deterministic (5 SVDs are reproducible) and is
   the literal product of the 5 single-block no-retrain rank truncations
   applied simultaneously: cycle 280 b1 r=2 init was 57.66; replacing 5
   c_proj operators at once accumulates ~96 PPL above float at init. Joint
   Adam over 46,080 params closes ~77 PPL of that delta in 4000 steps but
   is budget-bounded (final@4000 ~0.77 PPL above best_mean across seeds).

3. The composition pattern from cycles 284/291 does NOT extrapolate to
   5 blocks. Cycle 284 (b1 + b11) was non-destructive (+3.85 vs b1 alone,
   +0.59 below b11 alone) and the cycle 291 retrospective hypothesised
   "high-rank partners absorb low-rank damage". 5 concurrent anchors break
   that pattern: even with rank-22 (b11) and rank-24 (b3) "high slack"
   partners present, the 5-stack damage exceeds either single anchor by a
   factor far larger than the cycle 284 / 291 deltas would predict.

4. Cycle 304's prediction stands. Cycle 304's k_95 measurement showed
   only b11 is a TRUE rank-2 terminator and the "concentrator" label
   (cycle 302 panel 4 E_top2) was a rank-2 energy-mass artifact that does
   not coincide with curvature termination at b1/b2/b10. Cycle 304's
   audit explicitly predicted: "destructive composition (>55 PPL) is more
   likely than the item 315 best-mean-≤-51 CONFIRMED threshold". Cycle
   305 confirms this prediction at +18 PPL above the REFUTED bar.

5. The cycle 280/285/288/289/270 individual anchors were training-based
   rank selections (cycle 299: SVD init is 83% of optimal basin; Adam
   rotates row-space within rank-r and fits bias to close 8-15 PPL).
   When 5 such fits are jointly optimised, the 5 simultaneous bias +
   row-rotation constraints CONFLICT — the Adam basin around the joint
   SVD init does not contain a configuration that is simultaneously
   close to all 5 single-block optima. The 5 single-block floors are not
   a connected basin in joint parameter space.

6. Trainable params 46,080 vs sum of single-block budgets (2,304 + 2,304
   + 19,200 + 4,608 + 17,664 = 46,080): exactly the same total as if you
   trained each anchor independently and could just add them. The
   destructive composition is NOT a parameter budget shortage — there is
   exactly enough Adam capacity. The deficit is geometric: the joint SVD
   init is far from the joint optimum and Adam cannot reach the
   single-block floors from the joint init.

7. Practical implication: cycle 282-family single-block LASER anchors
   are NOT additively composable into a multi-block LASER recipe. Any
   future multi-block c_proj-rank schedule must either (a) raise the
   per-block ranks beyond the single-block floors to give Adam slack
   or (b) use sequential/coordinate descent over blocks rather than
   joint Adam from a 5-replacement init, or (c) use a curvature-aware
   composition that does not simultaneously truncate the SVD bases at
   all 5 blocks.
