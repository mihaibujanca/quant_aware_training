== Substantive findings (sharper than INCONCLUSIVE) ==

1. **M3 (joint Adam saddle) REFUTED.** Sequential Adam (phase A: only b10
   trains for 2000 steps; phase B: freeze b10 at A best, only b11 trains for
   2000 steps) lands at 61.21 PPL, which is +0.49 PPL ABOVE cycle 310's joint
   Adam best of 60.72. Sequential coordinate descent does NOT escape the
   destruction. The destruction is structural, not an optimisation pathology.

2. **M3 ablations (one-sided restores) cleanly reproduce single-block
   diagonals**, confirming the destruction is *purely interactive*:
     ablation A (b10 float + b11 r=22 only):  best = 54.7724
                                              cycle 270 b11 r=22 ref = 54.4687
                                              delta = +0.30 PPL (within budget tolerance)
     ablation B (b10 r=5 only + b11 float):   best = 51.4783
                                              cycle 282 b10 r=5 diag = 51.4659
                                              delta = +0.01 PPL (essentially exact)
   Both ablations sit at their own single-block anchors. Touching EITHER
   block alone is fine. Destruction requires BOTH blocks compressed
   simultaneously.

3. **M1 (residual stream redundancy) gives a split signal.** The mean
   principal angle between top right-singular subspaces of Δy_10 and Δy_11
   sits in the middle band (39.63 deg minimum across the 4 measurement
   variants), so it does not cross either threshold. BUT the leading
   principal angles tell a clearer story:
     raw Δy variant, top-5 cosines (5 SVs of b10 vs top-22 of b11):
       cos      = [0.967, 0.865, 0.718, 0.619, 0.539]
       angles   = [14.77, 30.09, 44.14, 51.79, 57.38]  deg
   The TOP-1 direction has cosine 0.967 (angle 14.8 deg) -- the leading
   direction b10 writes into the residual stream is almost identical to a
   leading direction b11 writes. The TOP-2 direction also overlaps strongly
   (cos 0.865, angle 30.1 deg). The tail (top-3..5) diverges. So the rank-r
   subspaces share a few strong directions but many incompatible directions.
   M1 is therefore PARTIALLY supported: the destruction is consistent with
   "the two latest concentrators write the SAME 1-2 leading directions and
   compressing both forces them into competition for the same low-rank
   slots", but NOT consistent with "the rank-5/22 subspaces are essentially
   the same".

4. **The curv variant ((GELU-ReLU)*W_proj, cycle 302/304 form) shows weaker
   alignment than the raw variant** (mean angles 56.3 / 67.5 / 71.4 deg vs
   raw 39.6 / 62.6 / 64.8 deg). The curvature residual subspaces are LESS
   aligned than the full FFN write subspaces. This means the redundancy is
   not concentrated in the GELU-vs-ReLU curvature signal -- it sits in the
   shared linear / mean structure of GELU(z) @ W_proj. Both blocks write
   SIMILAR leading directions in the residual stream regardless of the
   nonlinear gating, and that shared subspace is what is being squeezed by
   the rank-r joint compression.

5. **M2 sequential PPL trajectory is informative.** Phase A (only b10 r=5
   trains, b11 stuck at SVD-init r=22) recovers from init 76.65 down to
   64.67 in 2000 steps (still descending: best at step 2000). Phase B
   (b10 frozen at A best, b11 r=22 trains from its SVD init) recovers from
   64.67 to 61.21 in another 2000 steps (still descending). Compared to the
   M3 single-block ablations:
     phase A best (b10 fits, b11 frozen at noisy SVD init) = 64.67
     M3 ablation B (b10 fits, b11 at FLOAT)                = 51.48
     -> +13.19 PPL gap explained entirely by b11 sitting at its r=22
        no-retrain SVD init (which alone costs ~5.4 PPL above b11 r=22
        retrained anchor 54.47, plus the additional damage of compressing
        b10 on top of an already-corrupted residual stream).
     phase B best (b11 fits, b10 frozen at A best)         = 61.21
     M3 ablation A (b11 fits, b10 at FLOAT)                = 54.77
     -> +6.44 PPL gap. Even AFTER b10 has been Adam-fit to its best phase-A
        position, b11 cannot reach its single-block anchor; the b10
        compression poisons the residual stream that b11 receives. This is
        the core diagnostic: there is NO state of (b10_lowrank, b11_lowrank)
        in this rank budget that recovers each block's single-block PPL,
        regardless of optimisation order.

6. **The destruction is NOT joint Adam saddle (M3 refuted), is NOT a pure
   single-block budget shortage (M3 ablations succeed), and is consistent
   with M1-style "both blocks want to use the same top residual-stream
   directions and the rank budget is insufficient for the union of their
   needs" -- but the formal M1 angle threshold (<30 deg mean) was set on the
   full rank-r mean and the measured rank-r mean (39.6 deg) sits in the
   middle band, so the verdict per item 326 is INCONCLUSIVE.**

== Geometric object ==

LowRankCProj(rank=r):
  W_l   = c_proj_l.weight             SVD on CPU float32
  A_l   = U_l[:, :r] * S_l[:r]        (3072, r)  frozen buffer
  B_l   = Vh_l[:r, :]                 (r, 768)   nn.Parameter
  bias  = c_proj_l.bias               (768,)     nn.Parameter
  forward x @ (A @ B) + bias          matches HF Conv1D semantics

For M1, Δy_l = sigma(z_l) @ W_proj_l where z_l = ln_2(x_l) @ W_fc_l + b_fc_l,
captured on (200 calib seqs * 128 tok = 25600 tokens). Computed for two
sigma forms: (a) raw GELU(z), (b) curvature residual GELU(z) - ReLU(z).
Per Δy: SVD, take top-r right singular vectors as Vh. Principal angles =
arccos of svdvals(Vh_10[:k_a] @ Vh_11[:k_b].T). Variants:
  (5, 22)  (item 326 native: r_10 vs r_11)
  (5, 5)   (matched-rank symmetric)
  (22, 22) (top-22 of b10 vs top-22 of b11)

== Why INCONCLUSIVE per item 326's strict threshold ==

The item 326 verdict logic is a binary decision tree on
(sequential_ppl, mean_angle):
  M3-pathology      requires sequential_ppl <= 55 (got 61.21 -- fails)
  M1-redundancy     requires sequential_ppl >= 60 AND mean_angle < 30 deg
                    (got 61.21 OK; 39.63 fails)
  REFUTE-unknown    requires sequential_ppl >= 60 AND mean_angle > 60 deg
                    (got 61.21 OK; 39.63 fails)
  else              INCONCLUSIVE.

Measured (61.21, 39.63 deg) lands in the "else" cell. The strict verdict is
INCONCLUSIVE.

The substantive interpretation, however, is sharper:
  - M3 (Adam saddle)     REFUTED -- sequential >= joint within 0.5 PPL
  - M1 (redundancy)      PARTIAL -- top-2 dirs strongly aligned (14.8, 30.1 deg)
                                    rank-r mean in middle band (39.6 deg)
  - M2 (LN coupling)     UNTESTED directly; consistent with the data (an LN
                         coupling story would also predict that fitting b10
                         poisons b11's LN input, which is exactly what
                         phase B sees: b11 cannot reach its single-block
                         anchor with b10 in any low-rank configuration).

The PRINCIPLED conclusion: at the cycle-282-family ranks, the (b10, b11)
destruction is a STRUCTURAL interaction between the two latest concentrators
sharing the top 1-2 residual-stream write directions. It is not optimisation
pathology and not a single-block budget shortage. Whether to call it pure
M1, pure M2, or a M1+M2 hybrid requires a follow-up: e.g. a higher-rank b10
single-fit measurement or a direct LN-input subspace test.
