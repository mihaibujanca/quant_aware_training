== Substantive findings sharper than REFUTED ==

(1) The "flat W_proj + broad curvature residual" hypothesis is wrong as
    stated. b8's W_proj top-2/top-5 ratio (1.49) is essentially the
    concentrator median (1.51); b8's Dy stable rank (1.20) is barely above
    the concentrator median (1.08), not the 1.5x required.

(2) Δy is RANK-1 DOMINATED at every measured block on its OWN SVD basis.
    Stable rank ∈ [1.018, 1.310]; top-1 energy fraction ∈ [0.874, 0.991].
    The "broad curvature residual" reading of cycle 304 (b8 k_95 = 511) was
    a W_proj-BASIS PROJECTION ARTIFACT, not an intrinsic property of Δy.
    On Dy's own SVD, b8 k_95 = 73; b2 k_95 = 1; b9 k_95 = 110; b11 k_95 = 1.

(3) The actual b8 anomaly is now visible: b8 has the LARGEST gap between
    intrinsic Dy k_95 (73) and W_proj-basis-projected k_95 (511, cycle 304).
    Difference = 438 ranks. This is a MISALIGNMENT effect, not flatness or
    breadth: b8's nearly-rank-1 Δy points in a direction that does not
    project well onto Vh_w[:k] for small k. The remaining ~6% energy of Δy
    spreads diffusely across W_proj's right-singular tail when projected
    into that basis, but the energy itself lives in a low-rank intrinsic
    subspace of Δy.

(4) cos_top5_sum (principal-angle alignment of Vh_y[:5] and Vh_w[:5], max
    = 5) at b8 = 2.65 -- comparable to b1 (2.64) and b7 (2.61), and ABOVE
    b2 (2.12) and b9 (2.12). The misalignment is therefore TOP-1 specific,
    not a broad 5-frame mismatch: b8's first right SV of Δy (which holds
    91% of the energy) is the locus that fails to align with Vh_w[:2].
    b11 has the highest cos_top5_sum (3.08) and the highest E_kept_top_2
    (0.98), the expected coupling.

(5) Implication for cycle 315 multi-block LASER: item 316's CONFIRMED
    branch had argued "block 8 should NOT be added to any multi-block
    LASER stack -- its curvature residual has nowhere to hide". REFUTED
    flips the geometric reading: b8's curvature residual IS rank-1
    concentrated and would fit a rank-1 LASER replacement of c_proj_8 IF
    the rank-1 axis is chosen along Vh_y[:1] and not Vh_w[:1]. A LASER
    using Vh_w[:1] (the standard cycle 280/284/305 construction) would
    leave 53% of Δy energy unprojected, which is the cycle 305 result
    when block 8 is included and explains why cycle 305 destructive
    composition was strongest at the mid-blocks. The constructive
    follow-up is to test a Δy-aligned rank-1 LASER variant at b8.

(6) Cross-validation with cycle 302 / cycle 304 references:
    - E_kept_top_2_via_Wproj at every block matches cycle 302 panel 4
      to <= 1e-4 (asserted).
    - cycle 304 k_95 measurements (W_proj-basis projection) are NOT
