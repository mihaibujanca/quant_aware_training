## Key Findings

1. Early blocks (1-3) show 21-29% excess zone fraction over Gaussian prediction —
   active concentration beyond what variance alone predicts. Late blocks (7-11) match
   Gaussian within 5%.

2. Bump-dominated channels (σ<0.5, |μ|<0.5) peak at block 1 (44.4% of channels),
   falling sharply to near-zero by block 7. These channels are structurally "parked"
   in the GELU nonlinear knee.

3. Spearman ρ(σ_j, importance_j) is negative throughout (range -0.65 to +0.05),
   meaning low-σ (bump-dominated) channels tend to have HIGHER importance scores.
   Note: this correlation is partially tautological since importance includes std(h).

4. Mean σ increases with depth (0.52 at block 1 → 0.80 at block 11), consistent
   with pre-activations spreading out in later layers.

5. KS statistics (mean D over 128 sampled channels): range 0.028-0.076, confirming
   departures from Gaussianity are real but small. Early blocks have higher KS.
